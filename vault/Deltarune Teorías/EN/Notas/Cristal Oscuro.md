> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 💎 A secret key item in every chapter (original name: *Shadow Crystal*; in Japanese シャドウジェム, *Shadow Gem*, "Shadow Gem"). This note always uses "Cristal Oscuro"/"Shadow Crystal" so as not to mix languages.
> 🧮 There are **five confirmed to date**, one per chapter from 1 to 5, each obtained by defeating that chapter's secret boss — the wiki itself groups them under the unofficial name "Shadow Crystal boss," chosen by community vote on June 12, 2025.
> 🪟 In the Dark World it's technically invisible, but it still casts a shadow, as Seam explains. In the Light World it turns into a simple piece of glass called "Glass" — an item that can't be thrown away because [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "it would feel wrong to throw it away."
>
> ![[dark_crystal_Glass_item.png]]
> *The "Glass" in Kris's inventory — the form the Shadow Crystal takes when it crosses into the Light World.*
> 🔮 Using it inside a Dark World shows Kris a vision tied to that area's theme: a flash of the equivalent Light World location, a possible glimpse of the future (in the Cyber World/TV World), or even a vision of characters who aren't present at that moment (in the Garden). The effect is one-use-per-location and can't be repeated.
> 🚫 The tapestries/stained-glass windows in the Dark Sanctuaries that narrate the Prophecy (Ch.4) are **immune** to this vision effect — see [[Profecía|Prophecy]].
> 🗽 The word "Freedom" appears in the description or dialogue of almost every one of its bearers.
> 👤 Several bearers were contacted by the same mysterious figure, "Strange Someone" (Alguien Extraño), right before losing their minds.
> 💭 Ch.5 reveal: Seam explains that the Crystals contain [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "the power of lost dreams" — what's left of a strong attachment, a dream held onto tightly that ends up breaking. He calls them "failed dreams," useless to Kris... but promises he knows something "very, very interesting" to do with the full set of 5.
> 💾 Save mechanic: the chapter-select menu shows which save file has which Crystal. If a secret boss was defeated on a different save file, the Crystal (and the gear it drops) can be recovered by inspecting the hole west of Castle Town — but only for, at most, the two chapters immediately before the current one.
>
> ![[Shadow_Crystal_item_Castle_Town.png]]
> *Recovering a pending Shadow Crystal from the hole west of Castle Town.*
> 🛠️ Before v1.07 of the game, Jevil's Crystal did not physically appear in the party's inventory — Seam simply mentioned it when talking to him in Ch.2.

## What it is
A secret, recurring key item: each chapter from 1 to 5 hides an optional boss, tougher than the rest of the chapter, whose defeat grants a Shadow Crystal plus exclusive gear. Seam is the one who receives, studies, and keeps them as the Delta Team collects them — he is, by far, the main source of information about what they actually are.

## The Shadow Mantle — a sibling item, not a Crystal
The Shadow Mantle is an item **distinct** from the Shadow Crystals, but belongs to the same thematic circle: it reduces Dark/Star-type damage by 66% and makes the mandatory battle against the Roaring Knight at the end of Ch.3 much easier (though it isn't required to beat it). It's obtained by completing an optional S-Rank sidequest across the three minigame boards of Ch.3, which leads to its own miniboss, "Nightmare" — nicknamed by the fandom **ERAM**, and catalogued by the official wiki as "Shadow Mantle Holder." See [[ERAM]] for the full development of that battle, including the special defeat dialogue ("Typer 667") that only appears if the Mantle is equipped.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Who is "Strange Someone"?
> Several bearers (at least Jevil and Spamton) were contacted by the same mysterious figure before their decline. It isn't confirmed whether it's the same person in both cases, nor their identity — it's one of the fandom's most-cited open questions, and it has even been speculated that it could be related to the Voice/Gaster.

> [!question]- The "increasingly Lightner" pattern
> Strong theory: Shadow Crystal bearers become progressively less "purely Darkner" — Jevil is a normal Darkner, Spamton is halfway there, the Roaring Knight seems half Lightner, half Darkner, and Gerson is directly a deceased Lightner. Pink, the fifth bearer, fits in a different but related way: she's a Darkner (the Mew Mew Kissy Cutie doll) possessed by a Lightner ghost from Undertale — again, a Darkner that at its core "is" or contains a Lightner. It's still unconfirmed where this pattern is heading for Ch.6.

> [!question]- The "Pure Crystal"
> There's an unused item in the game's files called "Pure Crystal," whose internal description alludes to [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "the shadow purified by the cat" — a phrase that fits strikingly well with Seam (literally a stuffed cat) and with his promise of being able to do something "very, very interesting" with the full set of 5 Crystals. 🎲 It's cut content that was never implemented (so far), so this is a speculative reading, not a confirmation of exactly what that item would do.
>
> ![[ShadowCrystals.png]]
> *The five Shadow Crystals together — the complete set that, according to this theory, would allow the creation of the Pure Crystal.*

> [!question]- 🎲 The fallen star and the crystal tears
> A book in the 2nd Dark Sanctuary (Ch.4) describes a fallen star whose tears, made of a bitter water, formed something "like crystal." This is a possible symbolic origin for the material that both the Shadow Crystals and the tapestries/stained-glass windows that narrate the Prophecy are made of (see [[Profecía|Prophecy]]) — but the game never explicitly says both things are the same material, so this is a speculative reading based on a vocabulary coincidence ("like crystal"), not a confirmation.
>
> ![[shadow_crystal_lagrimas_cristal_teoria.webp]]
> *The exact text of the book in the 2nd Sanctuary [TODO: verify exact EN quote]: "And so the fallen star wept, forming rivers with its tears. Then, slowly, from the bitter water, something grew. It looked like crystal."*
>
> Several additional clues, cited by the community, reinforce the reading that this verse might refer to the Shadow Crystals themselves and not just the tapestries: examining the Shadow Crystal item, its description says [TODO: verify exact EN quote] that "a sharp shadow moves like water in your hand" — the same liquid image from the book — and its Light World form is called, precisely, "Glass." In a cut Ch.5 dialogue, leaked before release by Toby Fox himself on Twitter, Seam goes as far as to say the Crystals contain [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "a certain power... you could call it the power of 'will'... no, maybe 'will' isn't the right word. The power they contain is…" — a line that cuts off right there. Many assumed this would confirm that the Crystals hold the power of Determination, but when Ch.5 released it was revealed to be a red herring: the final dialogue talks about "lost dreams," not determination (see Quick facts, above). 🎲 An even more speculative branch points to the Roaring Knight himself as the "fallen star": he cries during his roaring animation at the start of the battle, and when defeated he drops a Shadow Crystal that is implied to have been part of himself or his sword — if the Knight is Dess (the dominant theory about their identity, see [[Roaring Knight]]), this would further connect with the fact that their room is decorated with star motifs and that Rudy describes them as a "musical prodigy," that is, a star in their own right. Neither of these last two readings is confirmed; they're honestly among the most speculative in this entire note.
>
> ![[Knight_overworld_reveal_crying.gif]]
> *The Knight's roaring animation, halfway between laughing and crying — the visual basis for the reading that connects them to the "fallen star."*

## Trivia
- The phrase [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "The air crackles with freedom" has appeared in the rotating descriptive text of several of these bosses.
- Before v1.07 of Deltarune, Jevil's Crystal did not appear in the party's inventory — Seam simply mentioned it when talking to him in Ch.2.
- Several bearers share the same musical leitmotif, nicknamed by the fandom "the Freedom motif."
- The name "Shadow Crystal boss" for the five secret bosses isn't official: it was chosen by the wiki community via vote on June 12, 2025, over other fandom nicknames that were also circulating.
- If a Crystal from a later chapter is handed to Seam before an earlier pending one, he redirects to the Castle Town hole — but it isn't possible to recover Crystals from more than two chapters back this way.
