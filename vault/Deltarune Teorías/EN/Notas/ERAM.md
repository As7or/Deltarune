*Table note: "ERAM" is a fandom nickname — its official name in the game's files is "Nightmare" (spr_shadow_mantle), and the official wiki catalogs it as "Shadow Mantle Holder."*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🗡️ Secret boss of the Sword Route in Ch.3, hidden behind the third S-rank minigame in the Original Game.
> 🖤 A shapeless black sprite with no confirmed official name — "ERAM" is a fandom nickname taken from the name of its battle music; in the files it appears as "Nightmare."
> 🧥 Carries the Shadow Mantle, a garment that reduces Dark/Star-type damage by 66% — highly recommended (though not mandatory) for the fight against the Roaring Knight at the end of the chapter.
> 🗣️ Speaks to Kris with an unsettling familiarity, showing broad knowledge of the Prophecy, the Dark Crystals, and the forbidden paths the Player can take — it refers to Kris's eyes as "flickering flames."
> 🏚️ Its battle takes place in an 8-bit recreation of the Shelter — the same place where the Knight took Undyne.
> 🎵 Its battle theme, "BURNING EYES," shares the "Battle Against a True Hero" motif from Undertale with "BIG SHOT," Spamton NEO's theme.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Enigmatic and malevolent, it seems fascinated — almost pleased — by the violence Kris can inflict on the game's hidden paths, mocking them for secretly enjoying it. It speaks with the slow, spaced-out cadence typical of the saga's "metanarrative" entities, similar to the Voice.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Is it the same as FRIEND?
> ![[ERAM_FRIEND_attack.png]]
> *ERAM summoning FRIEND as part of its attack pattern in battle.*
>
> They're easy to confuse because, during the battle itself, ERAM summons several FRIENDs as part of its attack pattern — it's not simply that they share the same room, but that FRIEND appears directly under its control, or as an extension of its own powers, at least in that context. Despite this, according to the official wiki they're separate entities: ERAM/Shadow Mantle Holder is the one who actually carries the Shadow Mantle and speaks with Kris, while FRIEND acts only as a support enemy summoned in that specific battle. Whether that control relationship holds outside the fight — whether FRIEND "works for" ERAM for the rest of the game — is never fully clarified.

> [!question]- Why does it share a musical motif with Spamton NEO?
> Its battle theme, "BURNING EYES," shares the "Battle Against a True Hero" motif from Undertale with Spamton NEO's "BIG SHOT" — a deliberate musical connection between the two bosses that the fandom reads as a nod to both representing, in different ways, the same kind of threat to Kris.

> [!question]- Is it actually Ramb?
> One of the most discussed theories. In favor: the breastplate Ramb wears visually resembles ERAM's face and shares its same orange mouth tone; Ramb claims to "stand guard" for Tenna without this ever being confirmed on screen, leaving unexplained where he really is during much of the chapter; and both share a similar background arc — it's speculated the Knight intended to give Ramb a Dark Crystal to turn him into another boss, just like Jevil or Spamton NEO, but arrived too late, since Ramb ends up petrified at the end of the chapter. **Fan theory, with several visual and narrative coincidences in its favor; unconfirmed.**

> [!question]- Is it the "video-game version" of the Roaring Knight?
> ![[Shadow_Mantle_holder_battle_flames.gif]]
> *ERAM wreathed in flames during its battle — the fire magic it shares with the Roaring Knight.*
>
> Both share the same musical leitmotif at the start of their battle themes ("BURNING EYES" and "Black Knife"), both are predominantly black creatures, and both wield fire magic — parallels that lead part of the fandom to read ERAM as a kind of reflection or "playable version" of the Knight within the Original Game, rather than as the Knight itself in disguise. **Fan theory, unconfirmed.**

> [!question]- Is it the "Strange Someone" who drove Jevil and Spamton mad?
> It's speculated that ERAM could be the same "Strange Someone" responsible for Jevil's madness and Spamton's decay, given its deep knowledge of the Dark Crystals and its active interest in pushing Kris toward the game's darker paths. After the battle, it remarks that both it and Kris "have work left to do" — a line the fandom reads as a possible allusion to continuing to open Dark Worlds and hand out Crystals to more Darkners. **Fan theory, speculative; unconfirmed.**

## Trivia

> [!question]- The etymology of "Nightmare" — why does it fit the Knight so well?
> Its real internal name, "Nightmare," contains the root "mare" — which doesn't come from the English word for a female horse, but from a distinct Germanic root meaning an incubus or spirit that causes sleep paralysis and nightmares, pressing down on the chest of the sleeper — also the real origin of the Spanish word "pesadilla." It's a general etymology fact (not specific to Deltarune), but it fits strikingly well with ERAM's dual nature: a nightmare tied to the night, and from there, in the fandom's reading, to the Knight itself (a partial homophone of "night").

![[Shadow_Mantle_holder_battle_idle.gif]]
*Its idle battle animation — its shapeless design is one of its most-discussed trademarks.*

- The fandom name "ERAM" comes directly from the name of its battle music track.
- Its internal file name, "nightmare_nes," is a possible nod to "We Were Born of Nightmares," the theme that plays before the battle against Shadow Nightmare in The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening.

> [!example]- The "Typer 667" dialogue when losing to the Knight (with Shadow Mantle)
> If Kris wears the Shadow Mantle obtained from ERAM and loses to the Roaring Knight, the text narrating the defeat uses a special narrator variant identified in the files as "Typer 667" (as opposed to the usual "Typer 666" seen in the rest of the game). Over successive defeats, the text becomes almost encouraging: it calls the defeat "very interesting," notes that Kris is "so determined" to try again, and upon losing a second time while wearing the Mantle, says it feels "your power shine a little brighter."
