> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🔌 A Darkner from the TV World representing a power strip from the Dreemurrs' living room — actually a Plugboy that Kris and Asriel took from the library as kids to play with like a toy.
> 🍸 He's the bartender of the Green Room; he grants access to the "Original Game" (a kind of prequel/parody of the Ch.2 Weird Route) to whoever gets an S, Z, or T rank on the chapter's boards.
> 🗝️ On the Sword Route, he's the one who lets you obtain the Shadow Mantle through the Original Game.
> 🪨 By the end of the chapter, he appears half turned to stone, unable to move — and if spoken to again, he reveals he saw Kris create the Dark Fountain and asks whether it was really a good idea.
> 🗽 He tells Kris, with calculated complicity: "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] But I know you, and boy do I know you... that's not what YOU want, is it, kid? Freedom. That's what YOU want" — the same keyword running through the entire Dark Crystal note.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> A grump with a heavy accent and the manners of a seasoned bartender, but with genuine fondness for Kris going back to his childhood: having been treated like a real toy, instead of being ignored like the rest of the Plugboys, made him feel special. He's seen as an outcast among the TV World's Darkners — a trait that, before Ch.3, was thought to be common to all Dark Crystal bosses.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — The Green Room bartender
> He appears as an NPC in the Green Room, offering access to the Original Game to whoever gets enough rank on the TV World's boards. He reminisces nostalgically about the old days of "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] Queen on one side, King on the other, Tenna behind," playing kings with the power strip that Kris and Asriel used as children. He also reveals that Kris and Noelle, as kids, very often played with several of the Darkners who would go on to be met in Chs.1 and 2 (like Lancer himself or Rouxls Kaard), using them like toy soldiers for rival armies — the real origin of why several of those Darkners already knew each other before the player arrived.

## Theories

> [!question]- Is he the bearer of the Shadow Mantle?
> Before Ch.3 was released, there was a lot of speculation that Ramb might be the actual bearer of the Shadow Mantle, given his outcast profile among the Darkners — a theory that was considerably weakened once it was revealed that role belongs to FRIEND, though part of the fandom keeps sharing art and theories about it purely out of fondness for the character.
