*Table note: Jackenstein is a Darkner native to the Dark Sanctuaries, with no Lightworld form. His pet Pumpkin has its own image further below, in the section dedicated to it.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🎃 Found in the Dark Sanctuary's library (Ch.4); his fight is fought in complete darkness.
> 🕯️ He's the Darkner version of a battery-powered decorative Halloween pumpkin kept in the town's own church — he doesn't represent a Light World character, but an object.
> 😢 He lives isolated in the dark because the other Darkners used to pick on him for his "big, ugly body"; he himself is afraid of how "creepy" his own face is.
> 💡 Introduces the SOUL's light mechanic: he can only be defeated using the "Unleash" ACT, which makes Kris's SOUL emit a bright light to chase and expose Jackenstein around the board — attacking him directly doesn't work.
> 🏆 His fight makes it clear, for the first time explicitly in the game, that Kris's soul and the Player's ("you") are two distinct entities — the light specifically comes from Kris's soul, not the player's.
> 🧱 His attacks trap the heroes in mazes: you have to find the key and the exit before Jackenstein loses patience ("YOUR TAKING TOO LONG") and enters the maze himself to catch them.
> 🏠 According to his own relationships wiki, he's "former roommate" of Gerson Boom (and possibly the Guei) inside the Dark Sanctuary — they share the same space before the group arrives.
> 🤝 After the fight, he helps the group climb a giant pillar — depending on the player's actions, he falls with the group and is presumed dead, or the rest of the recruited Darkners form a tower to lift them up instead.
> 🩹 Ralsei tries to heal a blow to his head after the fight without success, until the Old Man (Gerson) himself encourages Susie to try it herself — her spell does manage to reach the inside of Jackenstein's mask.
> 🕯️ What he wears on his head is a black-and-orange mask shaped like a Halloween pumpkin, capable of emulating expressions (it can smile, blush, or make its eyes glow orange); underneath it hides his real face — small and almost cute-looking, somewhere between a candle and a kitten, with pointed ears and an orange antenna/wick on the tip — hence his fear of being seen, and the real reason he needs complete darkness.

His fight takes place entirely in the dark, which prevents using FIGHT normally — only through the "Unleash" ACT can Kris's SOUL light up the board and chase Jackenstein to raise the Mercy meter. Ralsei can use "LightUp" to widen the radius of light, and on later visits Kris gets the "ScaredyCat" ACT to move faster through the mazes (at the cost of defense).

## Jackenstein's Pumpkin — his own "pet"
Besides Jackenstein himself, there's a separate NPC officially called "Jackenstein's Pumpkin": a black anthropomorphic pumpkin with a red outline, belonging to him, that appears during and after his fight as part of his attacks ("YOU'RE TAKING TOO LONG" while chasing the SOUL). It changes color depending on its expression — yellow if dazed or crying — and its body can stretch at will. It shares the same deformed speech pattern as Jackenstein, but rendered word by word instead of letter by letter, to sync with its voice lines. There are also similar but distinct pumpkins — a swarm of small red pumpkins and a "polite" pumpkin with a simpler expression — that shouldn't be confused with Jackenstein's personal pet.

## Trivia
- His name is a pun on "Frankenstein" applied to a Jack-o'-lantern-type pumpkin; his body design (stitching scars, riveted shoulder pads) directly recalls the classic Boris Karloff Frankenstein (1931).
- He follows the same naming pattern as other Deltarune bosses based on playing cards: Jevil is the Joker, King and Queen are their own cards, the Roaring Knight is the Knight — and Jackenstein represents the Jack.
- His "creepy" face under the mask is inspired, according to the community, by the design of the eponymous boss from *Sinistar* (1983), an arcade game where a giant face chases the player's ship if they take too long — the same concept behind his "YOU'RE TAKING TOO LONG" attack.
- His line "YOU DAIRE BRING LAIGHTS TO MY LAIRE!?" is almost a literal quote of a line from Ganon in the infamous cutscene before his fight in *Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon* (CD-i).
- Holding down the space bar after using "Unleash" reveals the SOUL light's hitbox — almost certainly a debug feature left in by accident in the final game.
- Several of his fight's visual elements, like the dancing orange pumpkins, had already been hinted at in one of the Halloween posts ("Roast Chicken") Toby Fox published on Bluesky in 2024, coinciding with Deltarune's sixth anniversary.
- A bug in early Ch.5 versions made Jackenstein die in the pillar scene regardless if the Titan Spawn was killed violently, even if the recruited Darkners' tower had saved him — fixed in v0.0.250.
