*Table note: Seam only appears inside his own shop (the "Seap") — he's the only shopkeeper in the game who never leaves it.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🧵 Darkner shopkeeper of Card Kingdom; a worn cat plush with a lavender face, brown clothing, and an orange plush collar.
> 🏪 He's only seen inside his shop, the "Seap" (pronounced "shop"), which moves from the Field (Chapter 1) to Castle Town (Chapter 2 onward).
> 💎 He's the one who receives, studies, and keeps all the Dark Crystals the Delta Team collects chapter by chapter.
> 🍵 He offers tea to the Delta Team, constantly cheers them on, and provides clues for finding Jevil and other key items.
> 🎩 His name is pronounced "Shawm," a nod to the pronunciation of "Sean" — just as "Seap" sounds like "Shop."
> 🃏 His role as court magician and Jevil's counterpart evokes cartomancy, where the black Joker is the Fool and the red one is the Magician.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Affable, upbeat, and always cheering on the Delta Team, with genuine fondness for Jevil despite having had to lock him up himself. His enthusiasm for the Dark Crystals grows chapter by chapter, going from initial caution to openly talking about "something very, very interesting" he could do with them.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The court magician
> He runs his shop in the Field of Card Kingdom. If Jevil has been defeated, Seam notices Kris got a Dark Crystal from him, and explains they could do something with these crystals if the group manages to gather more by defeating the secret bosses of each chapter.
>
> ![[Seam's_Shop_location.png]]
> *The location of his shop, the "Seap," in the Field of Card Kingdom.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — Move to Castle Town and the Shadow Mantle warning
> His shop moves to Castle Town following the Darkners' relocation from Chapter 1. If the Jevil and Spamton NEO crystals are obtained, he warns that the next enemy with a Dark Crystal (the Knight, revealed in Chapter 3) might be impossible to beat without an item called the Shadow Mantle — which he tries to give them ("If you had the SHADOW MANTLE, your luck might be different... no mantle, no crystal." [TODO: verify exact EN quote]), but he can't find it, implying someone has taken it from him.

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Hope
> On receiving Gerson Boom's crystal (Hammer of Justice), he remarks that, for the first time, he feels the group could actually win the battle ahead — a feeling he himself explicitly describes as "hope."
>
> ![[Seam_screenshot_premonition.png]]
> *One of the premonitions tied to the Dark Crystals that pass through Seam, already in Chapter 4.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — The revelation
> He finally openly explains what the Dark Crystals are, after hesitating between calling it "will" and deciding that word doesn't quite fit: they contain "the power of lost dreams... when a strong attachment forms, when a dream clings tightly and then breaks, these are the fragments left behind." [TODO: verify exact EN quote] He adds that those broken dreams are of no use to Kris, but that he knows something "very, very interesting" he could do with them, making clear that what's been said so far was just the tip of the iceberg.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Jevil
> His best and only friend in Card Kingdom — it was Seam who had to lock him up for his own safety, and who still asks after him chapters later. His role as court magician is, in a way, the exact counterpoint to Jevil's as the jester. [[Jevil]]

> [!quote]- Dark Crystal
> The only character who receives and studies each Dark Crystal as it's obtained; almost everything known about their nature comes from his comments. [[Cristal Oscuro|Shadow Crystal]]

## Theories

> [!question]+ What is the "very, very interesting" thing?
> In Chapter 5, Seam hints at knowing something he could do with the five gathered Dark Crystals, without ever saying what it is. It's one of the biggest open questions heading into the final chapters — some point to the unused "Pure Crystal" item (see [[Cristal Oscuro|Shadow Crystal]]) as the most likely clue.
>
