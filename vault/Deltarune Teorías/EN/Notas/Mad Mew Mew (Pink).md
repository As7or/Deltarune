> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 👻 A ghost from the Blook family —cousin of Napstablook and of Nobody/Mettaton, with whom she lives—, the same one who in Undertale possessed the training dummy as "Mad Dummy" and, in the Switch version, a Mew Mew animatronic as "Mad Mew Mew".
> 🏪 In Deltarune, the action figure she possesses is a gift from Alphys to Asgore, displayed as the mascot of his Flower King shop.
> 🌸 When the Flower Kingdom's Dark World forms in Ch.5, that same figure comes to life as Pink.
> 🎪 Before being revealed as the secret boss, she appears early in the chapter as a friendly (somewhat suspicious) shopkeeper in the Cliffs, calling herself "Big Sis" to Orange, who affectionately calls her "Ory".
> 🔑 She's the chapter's secret boss, hidden behind the pink door at Top of Castle — you need to buy the MysteryKey from her for 10 Pink Coins and clear a hidden platforming section to reach her.
> 💜 Her fight isn't won by attacking: she uses the purple SOUL mode (movement restricted to lines) and is immune to normal damage. You have to survive her patterns and use the ACTs Flirt / MegaFlirt / MegaFlirtX / GigaFlirt to fill the "Doki Meter" with pink hearts — 15 points trigger a dating-sim-style scene at the end of Phases 1 and 2, and 20 points end Phase 3 and the fight.
> 🏠 She lives with Napstablook and "Nobody" (probably Mettaton) in a house between Bratty's and Flower King's.
> 🏆 The saga's first female secret boss; she has, to date, Deltarune's most detailed dialogue portrait, and the vocals for her battle theme are performed by Hatsune Miku.
>
> ![[Cutie_Mew_Mew_Magic_soundtrack.png]]
> *Pink's battle theme, with vocals performed by Hatsune Miku.*

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Irritable and prone to assuming the worst intentions from those around her —a direct inheritance from her personality as Mad Dummy in Undertale—, with the characteristic verbal tic of repeating words three times with escalating intensity. Outside of battle, she's surprisingly calm and serves as a voluntary punching bag for anyone who asks. During the fight itself, she confesses that she felt incredibly lonely and isolated from the outside world without realizing it, until seeing a sunset, and promises herself she'll make "lots of friends" in the Flower Kingdom.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Alphys
> Gave Asgore the Mew Mew figure she ends up possessing as Pink — the indirect origin of everything that happens to her in Ch.5. [[Alphys]]

> [!quote]- Asgore
> Keeps the plush figure as a decorative mascot in his shop, with no suspicion at all of what it actually hides, until the Ch.2 Dark Fountain gives it life of its own as Mad Mew Mew. Their prior relationship is purely incidental — Asgore has no idea he's had, literally, a sleeping Darkner in his shop window. [[Asgore]]

> [!quote]- Seam
> He's the one who manages the game's five Shadow Crystals and who finally reveals their nature —[TODO: verify exact EN quote] "the power of lost dreams"— once Pink hands over the fifth and last one. [[Seam]]

> [!quote]- Shadow Crystal

> [!quote]- Kris
> She's considered a direct parallel to Kris: both are "vehicles" controlled by an external entity (the irritable ghost in one case, the Player/Angel in the other), both share a body with that entity, and in both cases the body itself has its own desires, distinct from whoever controls it. ## Theories

> [!question]+ The parallel with Kris
> The most-cited comparison about Pink: both she and Kris are a body shared between a will of its own and an external force controlling it, and in both cases that external force can end up separating from the "vehicle". It's one of the game's most direct readings of its own central theme of control, shown here in miniature through a side boss.

> [!question]- The structural mirror with Gerson Boom
> The fandom points out that Pink functions as the exact reverse of Gerson Boom, Ch.4's secret boss: in Undertale, Gerson is a shopkeeper who's never fought, while Pink is a mandatory miniboss as Mad Dummy and an optional boss in the console version as Mad Mew Mew; Gerson uses the green SOUL mode (with a shield), Pink the purple one (restricted to lines); Gerson is key to the Dark Shrines' plot, while Pink is irrelevant to the Flower Kingdom's main plot outside her own side quest; Gerson encourages Susie to trust her healing magic, while Pink's fight encourages the party to have her accept her own Darkner body as an equal; and Gerson disappears once the last Fountain is sealed, while Pink is invited to live in Castle Town.
>
> ![[old-man-gerson.png]]
> *Gerson Boom, the structural reverse the fandom compares to Pink.*

> [!question]- Was she meant to be Hotland's miniboss in Undertale?
> Given how many times Alphys mentions Mew Mew Kissy Cutie in Hotland, it's theorized that Mad Mew Mew was originally intended as that area's miniboss before being replaced by Muffet — and that Pink's fight could be, as a byproduct, a kind of resurrection of that discarded concept, similar to how the fight against the Hammer of Justice recovers the idea of a confrontation with Gerson.

> [!question]- A deliberate bait against the FRIEND theories
> Before her identity is revealed, Seam suggests in Castle Town that Kris equip the Maniquí (Dummy), armor with resistance to cat-type damage — a nod many read as a hint that FRIEND (another cat-themed enemy) would be Ch.5's secret boss. The fandom interprets that Mad Mew Mew was chosen as the last Shadow Crystal precisely as bait against that speculation, leaving the real connection between FRIEND and the Eggs for the later secret dialogue.

## Trivia
- In Undertale (Switch version), Mad Mew Mew is a secret miniboss that replaces Sans in the Dog Casino, or appears after defeating Mettaton EX, depending on the version.
- The name "Pink" refers to the color of the Mew Mew Kissy Cutie figure itself.
- There's an unused bomb attack in the game's files, with the diamond bombs from Chaos Bomb separated out; it never made it into the final version of the fight.

![[Pink_battle_bomb_attack_unused.gif]]
*Pink's discarded bomb attack, documented in the game's files but never used in the final fight.*

- Her bombs explode with the same four-cardinal-direction shockwave pattern as those of the Shadow Mantle Holder (ERAM); the fandom theorizes they deal so much damage because they aren't Cat/Dummy type, unlike the Tasques that the Tasque Manager prays will deal extra fire damage.
- The spring 2024 Cutscene/newsletter already hinted at her role in Ch.5: in that illustration, Mad Mew Mew appears buried up to her head and mulched alongside other colored flowers and Flowey, holding Papyrus's hand.
- One of the items she sells, the WoodBlade2, is a possible homage to the stick Frisk carries when first entering the Underground in Undertale.
- She's the second secondary boss to explicitly swear in the game, after Spamton NEO.
- One fandom reading connects her possessing a Mew Mew doll (instead of a life-size animatronic like in Undertale's Switch version) with the use of "doll" as an English-language colloquial term for trans women — a minority interpretation, not confirmed by the game or by Toby Fox.
