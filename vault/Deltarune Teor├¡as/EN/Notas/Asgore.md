> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🌻 Father of Kris and Asriel, ex-husband of Toriel; owner of the "Flower King" flower shop in Hometown.
> 🌷 Tritagonist of Ch.5, whose plot revolves around his own flower shop turned Dark World.
> 🏚️ Takes little care of himself (messy car, cluttered house) and owes rent to Mayor Carol Holiday, who lets it slide with generosity.
> 👮 Was Hometown's chief of police before Undyne, until he was fired for reasons the game never fully explains — one of the first visible symptoms of his personal decline.
> 📌 Has a secret documents corkboard in Flower King's basement, very similar to one that appears in Carol's bedroom.
> 💐 His 7 colored flowers, cared for over the years in the attic, come to life as the antagonists of Ch.5 (see Flowery).

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Careless about material things but affectionate and guilt-ridden emotionally — he carries an enormous weight of self-blame over a tragedy never fully revealed, which he believes cost him his marriage to Toriel. He's sentimental and nostalgic, something Flowery actively exploits to keep him distracted in Ch.5.
>

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The hug at the flower shop
> Kris can visit Flower King after school and finds Asgore watering the flowers. As soon as he sees them, he runs to hug them and lifts them off the ground with emotion, almost using the exact same words he uses when meeting Frisk in Undertale. Before they leave, he secretly gives them a bouquet to hand over to Toriel — she isn't fooled, but accepts it reluctantly if Kris is the one giving it to her.
>
> ![[Asgore_Dreemurr_overworld_hug.gif]]
> *Asgore hugging Kris the moment he sees them walk into the shop — his first in-person appearance in the game.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — Free pickles at Sans's shop
> The flower shop appears closed, but Asgore shows up the next day at Sans's grocery store, while Sans is joking around with Toriel. He tries to talk to her — asking what flowers would remind her of when they first met — and she gently turns him down, though she promises they'll talk about Asriel when he's "back next week." Sans half-jokingly advises him to "talk to his plants," and Asgore leaves with some free pickles Sans gives him.
>
> ![[asgore_store_toriel_cap2.webp]]
> *Asgore and Toriel crossing paths at Sans's shop, with Kris present.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — The rejection after the sermon
> After Alvin's sermon at the church, Asgore tries to reconcile with Toriel — she turns him down again, this time more coldly than in Ch.2. The flower shop is still closed, but inspecting the back door reveals, through a basement window, a research corkboard covered in documents — the same kind of corkboard Carol also has (see [[Shelter]]).
>
> ![[Asgore_and_Toriel_overworld_awkward.gif]]
> *The awkward reunion between Asgore and Toriel after the Ch.4 sermon.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — Festival Day
> Right at the start of the chapter, he fills Kris in on the Festival: he and Toriel were crowned King and Queen in a past edition, which is where the real name of his flower shop, Flower King, comes from. In that same encounter, he confesses to Kris that he hopes to get his old job back through Carol's mediation, worried that Toriel has to raise him alone on a teacher's salary — a small, hopeful gesture from someone trying to support his family from a distance.
>
> Asgore is the chapter's tritagonist. His flower shop, Flower King, hides a Dark World created from the golden flower of his wedding bouquet with Toriel — now sentient as Flowery. He spends much of the chapter distracted by Flowery while Kris and [[Susie]] try to seal the Fountain without him seeing.
>
> ![[Asgore_screenshot_pushing.png]]
>
> At the end of the chapter, after the battle against Flowery and the 7 Colored Flowers, Asgore confesses that he blames himself for a tragedy not fully revealed (strongly hinted to be tied to Dess's disappearance and the loss of his position as chief of police), and that no one in his family ever blamed him — but that his own self-hatred "consumed him," which ultimately drove Toriel away. He laments not having protected Kris better after discovering how dangerous the Dark Fountain really was, admitting he was manipulated, and guides Kris to the Fountain to seal it.
>
> > [!danger]- The secret corkboard
> > In Flower King's basement there's a corkboard full of documents — and a very similar one appears in Carol's bedroom. Asgore states, speaking to someone unidentified, that he's about to show Toriel and all of Hometown "what really happened" and that they'll finally believe him that he only wanted to "protect everyone." This connects him directly to the mystery of the Shelter and to the Holiday family.![[Flower_King_location_second_floor_Chapter_5.png]]

## Theories

> [!question]+ What is the tragedy he doesn't fully reveal?
> His Ch.5 monologue strongly points to Dess's disappearance and the loss of his position as chief of police as the event behind his self-blame, but the game never confirms it by name. A strong theory given the amount of environmental clues pointing in that direction, but with no direct textual confirmation.![[That_Day_soundtrack.png]]

> [!question]- Asgore's secret corkboard
> His documents board in Flower King's basement — nearly identical to Carol's — suggests they both share an unrevealed investigation related to the Shelter. It remains one of the biggest open questions after Ch.5.
> ![[Flower_King_location_second_floor.png]]

## Trivia
- He's the same character (and the same name) as in Undertale, ruling over both worlds as king of the monsters.
- His carelessness with material things (dirty car, messy house) contrasts sharply with how meticulous he is about keeping his secret corkboard.
