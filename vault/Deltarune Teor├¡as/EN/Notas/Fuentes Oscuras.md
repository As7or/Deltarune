> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🌊 Original name: *Dark Fountain* (JP: 闇の泉, *Yami no Izumi*). They are columns of dark energy that shape a Dark World wherever they open.
> 🔪 As Queen explains in Chapter 2, **any "determined" Lightner can open one** by projecting their will into a sharp object and plunging it into the ground — Darkners can't do it on their own, which explains why Queen has to coerce Noelle instead of opening one herself.
> 💡 When it opens: a bright beam of light emerges from the exact spot and quickly dissipates; afterward, an opaque black mist fills the room. If the room is adjacent to the Light World, the mist seeps through, and if a door is opened, black smoke can be seen coming out and rising into the sky — the same image that describes the discovery of the Shelter in Chapter 3 (see [[Shelter]]).
> 🐉 If too many Fountains are open at once, an apocalyptic event called **the Roaring** (The Roaring) is triggered, in which Titans born from the Fountains ravage both worlds alike — see [[Titan]].
> 🪞 "The Fountain's will" shapes the Darkners of its Dark World — but what exactly "will" means (the will of whoever opens it, of whoever inhabits it, or something more diffuse) is never fully explained.
> 🏛️ Ralsei states, back in Chapter 1, that only Castle Town's Fountain of Darkness (within the Dark World itself) "is supposed to be standing" [TODO: verify exact EN quote] — all others are, by definition, irregular.
> 🎬 The visual backdrop behind every Fountain is stock footage (`IMAGE_DEPTH`, a cropped, recolored photo of the sea) reused at several key moments: the intro when the Voice speaks, beneath the Knight during his Chapter 3 fight, the Prophecy tapestries in Chapter 4, the stained glass in the Hammer of Justice fight, and the background of the 3rd Sanctuary's library — the same file (always in all caps, a trait associated with the Voice) that fuels the "Shelter/Depths/Hell" theory from the Thank You survey (see [[Shelter]]).
> 🎞️ There's real in-game footage titled *"The Birth of a Fountain"*, shown on a projector — it documents the creation of the Library Fountain in Chapter 2.

## Who opened each Fountain
To date, the Roaring Knight, Kris, and Susie are the only confirmed entities capable of having opened a Dark Fountain. This section lists all known ones, in chronological order.

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The Eastern Fountain (school locker)
> Shapes the Card Kingdom. It's the only one of the early fountains whose creator **is never confirmed in the game** — not even the official wiki knows for sure. It's also the central focus of the "Kris is the Knight" theory (see below). Kris seals it with the power of the SOUL at the end of the chapter.

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — The Library Fountain
> Shapes the Cyber World, in the library's computer lab. **Confirmed: opened by the Roaring Knight**, sometime after Chapter 1 — Noelle and Berdly fall in without knowing it while studying. Kris seals it at the end of the chapter.
>
> ![[Dark_Fountain_overworld_projector_footage.gif]]
> *A clip from "The Birth of a Fountain" footage, shown during Berdly's presentation in Chapter 2 — it documents the creation of this very Fountain.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — The Fountain in Toriel's Living Room
> Shapes the TV World. **Confirmed: opened by Kris himself**, at night, at the end of Chapter 2 — he tears out his SOUL while Susie sleeps over at his house, and plunges the knife into the living room floor. It's the first Fountain the group finds already active from the very start of the chapter, not partway through the exploration.
>
> ![[Dark_Fountain_screenshot_TV_World_creation.png]]
> *The exact instant Kris, at night and without his SOUL, opens the Fountain in Toriel's living room — the birth of the TV World.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Four Fountains in a single chapter
> The chapter with the most confirmed Fountains at once:
> - **1st Sanctuary** (the church's own Dark Fountain): opened by the Knight, found already active at the start of the chapter.
> - **2nd Sanctuary**: opened by **Susie**, her first Fountain — she borrows Kris's knife, remembering what Queen said in Chapter 2 about determination, to slip into the Dark World hiding the Elder and the concert-hall code.
> - **3rd Sanctuary**: opened by the Knight, this time **inside another Dark World** — the first confirmed instance of this happening — with far more "leaves" than usual. A Titan is born from that Fountain.
> - **Old Church Fountain** (reopened in the Sermon Room): the Knight opens it again while Kris and Susie visit Castle Town — it restores the areas and characters petrified by the first church Fountain, except for Gerson Boom.
>
> ![[dark_fountain_3_sanctuary_titan_creation.png]]
> *The 3rd Sanctuary Fountain, the first confirmed to open inside another Dark World — a Titan is born from it in Chapter 4.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — Twin Fountains at Flower King
> **Two Fountains** open inside Asgore's shop, almost simultaneously:
> - The first, the chapter's main one (shapes the Flower Kingdom), is opened by **Kris** — he tears out his SOUL, hides it in a trash can, and enters through the shop's back door while Susie buys a bouquet.
> - The second is opened by **the Roaring Knight**, "right when Kris was sealing the first one" as Ralsei narrates it — it's the one he uses to capture Asgore. Most notably: the community has pointed out that this second Fountain is **visually identical** to the one Kris had just opened, with no perceptible difference — feeding directly into the "Kris is the Knight" theory (see below).
>
> ![[Dark_Fountain_screenshot_Flower_Kingdom.png]]
> *One of the two twin Fountains at Flower King — Noelle, Kris, and Susie (in silhouette) facing it.*

> [!example]+ Unknown date — The Shelter's Fountain
> Hometown's Shelter hides its own Dark World, confirmed in Chapter 3 — but **no one knows who opened that Fountain, or when**. Unlike all the others, it has never been sealed or explored beyond Undyne's Darkner transformation. It is, along with the Chapter 1 one, the mechanic's great unknown. See [[Shelter]] for the full breakdown.
>
> ![[Dark_Fountain_screenshot_shelter.png]]
> *Black smoke coming out of the Shelter's door in Chapter 3 — the same image used to describe any Dark Fountain opening, applied here to a place whose Fountain no one has ever seen open.*

King and Queen agree that he's the one who's lately been "going around opening new Fountains" [TODO: verify exact EN quote]. [[Roaring Knight]]

> The only character able to sense a Dark Fountain's presence before finding it, and the first to warn of the Roaring as a consequence of having too many open at once. [[Ralsei]]

> [!quote]- Queen

> Pressured by Queen to open a Fountain in Chapter 2; she stands her ground and refuses. ## Theories

> [!question]+ 🎲 Who opened the Chapter 1 Fountain?
> It's the most debated question in the entire mechanic: neither the game itself nor the official wiki confirms who opened the school locker Fountain. The two main suspects are **Kris** (who already demonstrates the ability to open Fountains on his own from Chapter 2 onward, and whose SOUL-less body acts suspiciously in several nighttime scenes) and **the Roaring Knight** (the default suspect for any irregular Fountain). The most-cited argument for Kris is that the game never explains how to open a Fountain until Queen does so in Chapter 2 — after the Chapter 1 one already exists — which some read as a clue that Kris already knew how beforehand. The counterargument is that Ralsei only detects the Library Fountain upon reaching it with the group, without warning beforehand during the Castle Town visit, which could mean that particular Fountain wasn't yet open — or that Ralsei wasn't entirely honest. Still unresolved.

> [!question]- 🎲 The twin Fountains of Chapter 5 and the "Kris is the Knight" theory
> The most-cited detail from the fandom after Chapter 5: the second Flower King Fountain, the one the Knight opens to kidnap Asgore, is visually identical to the one Kris had just opened seconds earlier — same size, same look, no recognizable variation. Those who argue that the Knight and Kris are, in some way, the same entity (or share an identical will) point to this detail as the strongest visual evidence to date; skeptics respond that all Fountains of the same Dark World type might simply share the same default look, without that implying anything about who opens them. Related to the Kris-Knight cooperation theory already covered in [[Roaring Knight]].

> [!question]- 🎲 The Shelter's Fountain, the big loose end
> Unlike any other, no one has ever seen the moment it opens, no one knows who did it, and it has never been sealed or explored beyond a glimpse of Undyne's Darkner form. It is, along with the Chapter 1 unknown, one of the biggest unresolved mysteries in the whole series — see [[Shelter]] for the full breakdown of every theory surrounding it.

## Trivia
- The official wiki numbers the Fountains by order of on-screen appearance (up to 7 different ones in Chapter 4 alone, counting reopenings), a different criterion from the chronological "who opened it and when" order this note uses.
- The name "Eastern Fountain" comes literally from how Ralsei describes it while summarizing the Prophecy in Chapter 1 — it's not an internal game label, but prophetic terminology.
- Ralsei himself nearly stopped Berdly from opening a Fountain by explaining the Roaring to him — the same explanation that, chapters later, still doesn't stop the Knight from continuing to open his own.
