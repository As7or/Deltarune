## Kris and Susie's teacher.
[[Alphys]]

*Table note: Alphys has no confirmed Darkner form of her own — she always appears as a Lightner, in her role as teacher.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 👩‍🏫 Teacher of Kris, Susie, Noelle, Berdly, Catti, Jockington, Monster Kid, Snowy, and Temmie's class.
> 📼 She's still an absolute fan of "Mew Mew Kissy Cutie," the in-universe anime she already loved in Undertale — she even has very strong opinions on why the second season is better than the first.
> 🌷 She receives flowers from Asgore every time he asks her how Kris is doing in class — hinting that the two are friends.
> 🚲 She has a bicycle accident in Ch.2, which sparks Undyne's concern for her.
> 🤝 Unlike Undertale, Alphys and Undyne are practically strangers at the start of the game — their friendship is built from scratch over the course of the series.
> 💊 She worriedly remarks that she hopes "the internet gets fixed soon" because she doesn't want to "go back to her original form" — a direct reference to her amalgamate form in Undertale.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Shy, insecure, and with the same overflowing passion for geek culture (anime, video games, FPS podcasts for falling asleep) as in Undertale. She genuinely cares about her students, especially Kris, and doesn't hesitate to tactfully step in if she notices something off about his behavior.
>

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapters 1 and 2 — The teacher
> She teaches class as usual and can be found in the alley between the apartment buildings after school hours, where she admits she was worried when Kris and Susie didn't come back to class.
>
> ![[everyman_wall_paint.png]]
> *Alphys in Hometown's alley, next to the Everyman graffiti and the trash can full of well-kept flowers that can be examined in that same scene.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Undyne's invitation
> It's revealed that Undyne invited her to the church the night before, a sign that their friendship keeps progressing on its own despite the near-total lack of prior contact between them before the game.

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — The haunted house
> During the Festival, the church gets decorated for Halloween and turns into a haunted house. Alphys appears in costume, alongside Alvin, Catti, and Vegetoid — and actively takes part in the scare: she drops from the ceiling to startle the group, making Noelle scream. Afterward she admits, laughing, that it's her first year working there, which is why Noelle wasn't expecting her, and remarks that the church looks like "a hurricane hit it," referring to the damage from Ch.4.
>
> ![[Alphys_overworld_vampire_attire_spin.gif]]
> *Alphys in her costume for the church's haunted house, during the Ch.5 Festival.*

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Kris
> His teacher at Hometown school during Ch.1 and Ch.2; she isn't a figure who's very attentive to her students' individual details, but she does notice Kris's increasingly erratic behavior and mentions it to Toriel out of genuine concern, not as a complaint. She's one of the few adult figures outside the immediate family who picks up on something being wrong with him. [[Kris]]

> [!quote]- Undyne
> Unlike Undertale, they barely knew each other at the start of the game — their friendship grows from scratch: Undyne worries about her bike accident in Ch.2, even sending her an apology box of chocolates through Kris, and invites her to the church before Ch.4. Alphys, for her part, blushes as soon as the topic of the police comes up, and half-admits that "it's no secret they're easy on the eyes" before changing the subject, embarrassed. In Ch.5, with Undyne still missing at the Shelter, she asks Kris to find out whether Undyne isn't answering her because she's mad at her. [[Undyne]]

> [!quote]- Toriel
> Friend and colleague at school; Toriel often asks her how Kris is doing in class and whether he's been "acting strange." Alphys also tells Toriel things about Susie, like her habit of eating chalk. [[Toriel]]

> [!quote]- Asgore
> Friends: he often gives her flowers in exchange for news about Kris's performance in class, and they share their fondness for "Mew Mew Kissy Cutie." [[Asgore]]

> [!quote]- Mad Mew Mew (Pink)
> She gave Asgore the Mew Mew action figure that he keeps in his shop as a mascot — the same object that, in the Dark World of Ch.5, becomes the secret boss Pink. [[Mad Mew Mew (Pink)]]

## Theories

> [!question]- 🥛 Is Alphys the one leaving food by the alley's trash can?
> In Hometown's alley, where Alphys can be found after school hours in Ch.1 and Ch.2, there's a small bowl of food on the ground — right next to the trash can "full of well-kept flowers" and the [[Everyman]] graffiti. The fandom connects this detail to [[FRIEND]], the feline entity stalking the game: if someone leaves food there regularly, Alphys is the most obvious suspect, being the only person known to frequent that alley. **Speculative fan theory: the bowl is never explained in-game, and there's no confirmation it's related to FRIEND or that Alphys is the one who leaves it there.**
>
> ![[everyman_wall_paint.png]]
> *The bowl is visible on the ground, to the left of Alphys and Kris, in the same alley where the Everyman graffiti appears.*

> [!question]- 🐈‍⬛ Is she the hidden creator behind the mod that evokes FRIEND in Cat Petterz 2?
> FRIEND's full silhouette — a black shape with five feline legs — closely resembles Endogeny, the dog amalgamate from Undertale that Alphys herself created by fusing the Underground's named dogs; the community wiki notes the structural resemblance between the two, swapping only canine for feline (see [[FRIEND]]). Outside the game, in Noelle's real-life blog during the Spamton Sweepstakes, the entry about Cat Petterz 2 ("/catpetterz/") mentions, as an example of "how far things can go" with pet modding, a mod by a user named **"Mewmewqueen"** — literally "Mew Mew queen" — titled **"Sympton family pack."** Both names point directly at two of Alphys's already-confirmed hobbies: 📼 "Mew Mew Kissy Cutie," the anime she still adores in Deltarune, and 📺 "The Symptons," the sitcom whose DVD collection she lends to Kris (see Trivia). Add to that the food bowl in the alley, and much of the fandom speculates that Alphys could secretly be the person behind "Mewmewqueen" — and that her taste, both in and out of the fiction, for raising and modding rare pets might not be a coincidence next to FRIEND's own design.
>
> ![[alphys_friend_theory_comparation_amalgamate.png]]
> *A fan-made comparison: two versions of Alphys, each pointing to the amalgamate she'd evoke in her respective game — Endogeny in Undertale, and FRIEND's feline silhouette in Deltarune.*
>
> **Highly speculative fan theory: neither the game nor the real blog ever confirms that "Mewmewqueen" is Alphys — it's a connection built on shared names and hobbies, not a textual reveal.**

## Trivia
- She lends Kris her home DVD collection of "The Symptons," the sitcom about a middle-class monster family.
- Her gift to Asgore — the Mew Mew figure that gives rise to Pink in Ch.5 — unknowingly makes her the indirect origin of one of the most talked-about secret bosses in the whole game.
- Unlike Undertale, where she and Undyne have been a couple for years, in Deltarune they don't seem to know each other at all at the start of the game — Deltarune doesn't continue or depend on the relationships already established in Undertale; each game sets them up on its own (see [[Conexión Undertale|Undertale Connection]]).
