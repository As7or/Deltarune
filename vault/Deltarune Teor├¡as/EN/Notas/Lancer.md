> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🃏 Son of the King and prince of the Card Kingdom; Ch.1's secondary antagonist, turned close ally and friend of Susie.
> 👑 According to Seam, he's not just a decorative prince — the King put both himself AND Lancer in charge of the kingdom after locking up the other three Kings — he's technically a co-ruler, even though in practice the King ignores him almost entirely.
> 😈 He presents himself to Kris and Susie as "the bad guy," though he turns out to be clumsy, innocent, and desperate for real friends.
> 👊 His friendship with Susie begins when she scares him and he starts admiring her — they briefly team up together against Kris and Ralsei.
> 🎒 Starting in Ch.2 he becomes an inventory item, traveling in Kris's pocket; he can visit the Cyber World with the group but turns to stone if he spends too long there, being incompatible with that Dark Fountain.
> 🧢 He considers Rouxls Kaard his "lesser dad," since Rouxls is the one who actually looks after and feeds him despite the King's indifference.
> 🎉 He appears after the Ch.5 credits helping Susie decorate Castle Town for Noelle's arrival, alongside Queen —whom he calls "girldad"— on lounge chairs by the rooms Ralsei built for his friends.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Enthusiastic, naive, and completely unable to keep up the villain act for long — above all, he wants to be loved and taken seriously. He aspires to be as intimidating as Susie, whom he deeply admires. Despite his initial loyalty to his father, he's one of the few characters who treats Rouxls Kaard with genuine affection.
>

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Susie
> His best friend. They met as fake rivals/allies in Ch.1 and forged a genuine bond that stays strong chapter after chapter — she carries him piggyback, protects him, and he admires her deeply. [[Susie]]
>
> ![[Susie_and_Lancer_overworld_high_five.gif]]
> *Lancer and Susie high-fiving — the image that best sums up their friendship.*

> [!quote]- King
> His father, an abusive, indifferent ruler who rarely spends time with him or cares about his well-being — though he does care how he's treated publicly, and his threat to throw him off the roof turned out to be a bluff he'd never have gone through with. [[King]]

> [!quote]- Queen
> His mother, although Queen herself admits their relationship was "decided arbitrarily" the day the subject came up — it's unclear whether there's any real biological relation. Lancer calls her "girldad." [[Queen]]

> [!quote]- Rouxls Kaard
> His "lesser dad" (as Lancer himself calls him) — the one who actually looks after and feeds him day to day, while the King ignores him almost entirely despite having self-proclaimed himself co-ruler alongside him. Despite the dismissive attitude Rouxls Kaard puts on in public, Lancer is one of the few characters who treats him with genuine affection. [[Rouxls Kaard]]

## Theories

> [!question]- Why is Queen his mother?
> Queen herself deliberately leaves the relationship ambiguous — Lancer goes as far as to describe her as "a girldad, if you will" when asked whether Queen is his mother, without fully clarifying whether there's a real family bond or it's just a symbolic/adoptive one. The game never fully resolves it.

## Trivia
- He's incompatible with the Cyber World's Dark Fountain because he comes from a different Fountain (Card Kingdom), which slowly turns him to stone if he spends too long there.
- His introduction line, simply "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] I am... the bad guy!", is one of the fandom's most-quoted and most-parodied.
- According to Kris, it's hard to tell where his body ends and his clothes begin — it's not even clear whether he's wearing clothes or if that's just his natural appearance.
- He was designed by kanotynes (the same artist behind Jevil, Rudinn, Hathy, Clover, and King) in 2012, six years before Ch.1's release, as part of a series of 52 playing-card designs for a design class — Lancer was born as the Jack of Spades card. When asked what he was supposed to be, even she wasn't sure, though she suggested he could be some kind of Kewpie doll.
