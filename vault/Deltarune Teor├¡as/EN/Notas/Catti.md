*Table note: Catti has no confirmed Darkner form or talksprite.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🐈 Younger sister of Catty (the one who returns from Undertale); classmate of Kris and Susie in Alphys's class, and Jockington's girlfriend.
> 💬 Speaks in a monosyllabic, disconnected way, almost always looking at her phone, giving vague answers.
> 🎪 Met Jockington in her first gym class, on hula-hoop day, when the school ran out of hoops and she used him instead.
> 🌳 In Ch.4, her whole family gets trapped in a tree — Jockington helps her try to get them down, and Napstablook (who took part in a similar rescue in the past) was also trapped that time.
> ❌ Considers Susie "the enemy" and scolds Kris for spending time with her.
> 👪 Has an older sister, Catty, who returns from Undertale — a minor NPC with no note of her own in the vault, included here only as a family reference.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Distant and disconnected on the surface — always glued to her phone — but capable of surprising strong reactions when something really matters to her, like her open rejection of Kris hanging out with Susie.
