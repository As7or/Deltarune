> 🥚 He used to be the "White Addison", the smallest of the Addisons — spam salesmen with no success whatsoever.
> ☎️ His rise and his fall are both tied to phone calls from a mysterious person.
> 💔 Tenna's former partner and now archenemy; he actively protects Mike, the only person he doesn't want to hurt.

## Who he is
A homeless, erratic, and unstable salesman from the Cyber World; main antagonist of Chapter 2 and secret boss as **Spamton NEO**. He sleeps in a dumpster in a seedy alley, from which he emerges to try to make deals with anyone who passes by — including Kris.

![[spamton_home.webp]]
*The alley and the dumpster where Kris first finds him — all that's left to him after his fall.*

## Before the chapters — origin and rise
All of this is pieced together from dialogue and items found in Ch.2; it isn't shown on screen in real time, but reconstructed as a flashback scattered across the alley itself and Queen's Mansion.

**Origin: a failed Addison.** Before all of this, Spamton was the "White Addison", the smallest of a group of salesmen called the Addisons — mail spam creators that nobody ever looked at. Despite having no success at all, he always proclaimed to his fellow Addisons that he'd be a "big shot" someday. Desperate, he started looking for any way to become popular.

![[spamton_adison.png]]
*Spamton as the "White Addison", before his rise — the smallest and most insignificant of the group.*

**The call.** One day, he made phone contact with someone who could help him — and suddenly began having overwhelming success. From then on, Spamton was "on the phone all day", helped by that unidentified person.

![[spamton_telephone.gif|200]]
*The call that changed everything — the same phone that will later reappear, already disconnected, in Room Z of the TV World.*

He sold everything: TV ads, cars (the [TODO: verify exact EN name] "Cungadero", from "Big Shot Autos"), and became close with the butler Swatch, which got him a room in Queen's Mansion — where he discovered the powerful machine in the basement and started praying to it.

![[spamton_oldposter.jpg]]
*A "Big Shot Autos" advertising poster from his heyday, found broken in his own alley.*

![[spamton_room_mansion.webp]]
*His old room in Queen's Mansion, obtained thanks to his friendship with the butler Swatch.*

![[spamton_swatch.png]]
*Spamton wearing a disguise that mimics Swatch — according to one theory, a later attempt to sneak back into the Mansion without being recognized.*

His old Addison companions, envious of his sudden success, stopped hanging around him — something Spamton interpreted as proof that they never truly cared.

**Mailman, Tenna, and the broken partnership.** During his golden era, he became friends with TV host Tenna and his employee Mike, helping each other gain popularity (he even lent Tenna a plush "Pipis" — the same one Spamton will recognize in Ch.3, see below). Tenna refers to him dismissively as his "little MAILMAN", and they were planning a contract that would turn Spamton into a full-blown TV star.

![[spamton_tvworld_look.png]]
*The look he's implied to have had at the peak of his popularity: a red suit and yellow tie, a direct nod to Tenna himself.*

On the day Spamton was supposed to sign the contract that would change his life, he got a distressed call from the same mysterious person who had always helped him — asking him to leave the TV World. Spamton ran off, abandoning both his place in Queen's Mansion and Tenna's trust, with no explanation at all. Mike even left the TV World trying to find him.

**The garbage noise.** From that moment on, his success collapsed as fast as it had arrived. He ended up homeless, penniless, and, according to himself and the other Addisons, lost his mind — whether because of the ruin itself or because of his relationship with the mysterious voice on the phone. His way of speaking became erratic, full of brackets and substituted words, almost like a badly translated spam email. After his disappearance from the TV World, he also lost his room in Queen's Mansion — left without any of the places that used to house him, and ending up in the alley where Kris finds him.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Tenna
> Former business partners and now archenemies. Spamton hates him more than anyone else in the game — there are whole pages of accumulated resentment against him in official promotional materials, blaming him for having "abandoned" him. Ch.5 flips the script: it's Tenna who shows remorse, acknowledging that he knew the contract shouldn't be signed and pressuring him into it regardless. [[Tenna]]
>
> ![[spamton_tenna_enemies.webp]]
> *Spamton and Tenna, already declared enemies.*

> [!quote]- Mike
> The only person Spamton doesn't want to hurt in his quest for revenge — he actively protects him. Mike, for his part, tried to warn Tenna not to pressure Spamton into signing, and even left the TV World trying to find him after his flight. [[Mike]]
> ![[spamton_mike.webp]]

> [!quote]- FRIEND
> His yellow and pink glasses (which sometimes swap colors) match exactly with FRIEND's eye colors. Ch.5 confirms why: Pink reveals that "FRIEND cats" are so terrifying that people change their own appearance to avoid them — implying that Spamton wears those glasses and equips several pieces with anti-cat defense precisely to camouflage himself and protect himself from FRIEND, who could be actively hunting him. [[FRIEND]]

> [!quote]- Kris
> Spamton tries to buy Kris's SOUL under the promise of turning them into a "BIG SHOT", and on the Weird Route he precisely predicts the outcome of his pact with Kris. [[Kris]]

> [!quote]- ERAM (Shadow Mantle Holder)
> The theme for his fight against ERAM on the Sword Route, "BURNING EYES", shares the "Battle Against a True Hero" motif from Undertale with "BIG SHOT", Spamton NEO's own battle theme — a deliberate musical connection the fandom reads as both representing, in different ways, the same kind of threat to Kris. Both are also candidates for being the "Strange Someone" who pushes Shadow Crystal bearers toward madness. [[ERAM]]

> [!quote]- Everyman
> Shares with him the nature of a nod tied to the saga's real merchandising: Everyman appears non-canonically in the fake reviews and donations of the Spamton Sweepstakes, the real charity event associated with Spamton. [[Everyman]]

## Theories

> [!question]+ Room Z in the TV World, his old office?
> In Ch.3's TV World there's a room accessible only with Z rank (the lowest possible, so it has to be earned on purpose) that hides a very direct clue about Spamton's past: inside there's a black rotary phone **identical** to the one in his own Cyber World shop, with the receiver hanging off the hook and disconnected — the same detail both the Addisons and Tenna himself mention when talking about how Spamton "abandoned" his collaborations without explanation. The room also has a broken mirror and two commemorative "two stars" posters that, by context, seem to depict Spamton and Tenna together in their golden era.
>
> ![[spamton_z_room.webp]]
> *Room Z in the TV World: the disconnected phone, the broken mirror, and the "two stars" posters that suggest this was Spamton's old office.*
>
> The wiki's most widespread reading (strong theory, not direct confirmation): Room Z was literally Spamton's office or dressing room within the TV World before his falling-out with Tenna, and it stayed abandoned exactly as he left it the day he disappeared — a small, frozen museum of his time as a star.

> [!question]- Is the "person on the phone" Gaster?
> One sound clue associated with Spamton's fall is the line "there was nothing but garbage noise" — the same kind of meaningless static also associated with Gaster's communication attempts in Undertale. This has led part of the community to wonder whether the "person on the phone" who ruined Spamton and the entity behind Deltarune's Voice could be the same thing. Strong theory, but unconfirmed.

> [!question]- The "Puppet/Cat" element
> According to Spamton's own terminology in an official Q&A, there's a hidden element called "Puppet/Cat" (Element 6). Several of his items —the Dealmaker glasses, the FrayedBowtie, the TrueTie (a tie he's implied to have worn himself before)— reduce damage taken from this type of attack. It's mechanical, not just aesthetic, proof that Spamton actively protects himself from something shaped like a cat.
>
> ![[FRIEND.png|200]]
> *FRIEND — the most-cited candidate for the "cat" side of the hidden element Spamton protects himself from.*

> [!question]- More scattered answers from the 2022 Q&A
> In that same Spamton Sweepstakes Q&A, Spamton confirms or hints at several other things: that he was the one who sent Noelle the email with the virus the Pipis would be born from (see [[Pipis]]); that he and Jevil were friends before "he tricked him"; that he stole Lancer's swatch named "Lucas"; and, more intriguingly, that he himself has no idea who the Roaring Knight is —something that contrasts with his in-game dialogue, where he starts suffering right when he's about to tell us something about him (see [[Roaring Knight]]). About Mike, he answers with a question —whether whoever's asking is from the "Cat Crew"— and implies Mike doesn't belong to that group, asking that they "leave him alone". He also mentions, alongside the Puppet/Cat element, other possible elemental pairs without clarifying who they belong to: Darkness/Star and Death/Scythe, the latter speculated as a possible element for Jevil.
>
> ![[spamton_telephone.gif|200]]
> *The phone, the silent protagonist of much of his story — and of the Q&A itself.*

> [!question]- Why does he have a puppet's shape instead of a normal Addison's?
> All Addisons are the same size as each other, but Spamton is noticeably smaller and has a ventriloquist dummy/puppet design instead of a normal Addison's usual appearance. He himself implies that the Swatchlings once threw him into the acid river running through Queen's Mansion —Queen states that acid shrinks anyone submerged in it, and Spamton NEO's strings are an almost identical green to the acid itself— but neither the game nor Spamton himself fully explain why he ended up with this particular shape. It remains one of the character's unresolved design mysteries.
>
> ![[spamton_theory_acid_small.png]]
> *Community visual summary: Spamton's shop line about the acid, and Queen's comment that it "shrinks" you, in her Mansion's own acid tunnel.*

## Trivia
- He's visually inspired by 90s used car ads and by Deltarune's own real-world merchandising (Big Shot Autos exists as a brand both inside and outside the game).
- The Spamton Sweepstakes (a real merchandising event) has been used as a source of narrative clues about Ch.5, something unusual for a merchandising product.
- Toby Fox voices Spamton as well as Jevil, Queen, Tenna, and [[Flowery]].
- His shop's phone is directly inspired by the one in EarthBound, another of Toby Fox's favorite games.
