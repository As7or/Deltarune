*Table note: Clam Guy is the one that actually appears in Deltarune — not to be confused with Clam Girl, a different NPC exclusive to Undertale (Waterfall) with whom he shares an almost identical design. Curiously, in the game's internal files, both sprites are cataloged under the same name "Clam Guy".*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🐚 A character returning from Undertale (he lived next to the Floor 1 entrance of Hotland, near the water cooler) — in Deltarune he appears on the Hometown Lake path, in Ch.2.
> 👻 He only appears if Kris didn't interact with [[Onionsan]] in Ch.1, or in a new save with no carryover from Ch.1.
> 💬 He recalls waiting yesterday at the "weird circle" and being rude to "the thing" he saw there — a direct allusion to Onionsan. He assumes it won't come back now.
> 💚 He likes the color green.
> 👯 With the clam closed, his sprite is practically identical to Clam Girl's, an Undertale NPC who never actually appears in Deltarune — the most-cited visual connection linking the two characters despite officially being two distinct NPCs.
> 🕯️ That same Clam Girl is the one who, in the Switch/Xbox version of Undertale (released a month before Deltarune Ch.1), mentions a neighbor named "Suzy" and disappears with the same sound effect as Mystery Man and Gaster's Followers — the real basis for the entire theory connecting this character to Susie and Gaster (see Theories, below).

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> A minor, anecdotal NPC with very little characterization beyond his brief dialogue — his real interest to the community doesn't come from his personality, but from sharing a design with Clam Girl and, by extension, with all the "Goner" symbolism tied to Gaster.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — The Lake path
> He appears on the Hometown Lake path, oddly recalling that he waited the day before (Ch.1) by the "weird circle" — the spot where Onionsan usually is — and was rude to "the thing" he saw there. He assumes that, since it's no longer "yesterday," that thing won't appear again.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Did Gaster know Susie existed before Deltarune existed?
> If Clam Girl is really tied to Gaster (through the shared sound and color), her mention of "Suzy" would imply that, within the fiction itself, some version of Gaster was already aware of Susie's existence — and by extension, of all of Deltarune — even before the game was announced. It's one of the most-cited meta-narrative clues in the whole saga. **Fan theory, with a very solid basis of in-game clues; not confirmed by name within the game.**

## Trivia
- If Undyne is dead in the imported Undertale save, Clam Guy never appears in Hotland.
- Talking to Clam Girl in Undertale about Suzy unlocks a drawing in Sans's workshop with the inscription "don't forget" — the title of Deltarune Ch.1's credits song. If you haven't talked to her, the drawer instead shows a different photograph, tied to the Pacifist ending.
- Despite sharing a design, Clam Guy and Clam Girl are credited as separate characters in both games — the confusion between the two is almost as common among fans as the one between Catti and Catty.
