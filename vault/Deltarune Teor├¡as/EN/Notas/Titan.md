> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🌑 Primordial entities of pure darkness that emerge from a Dark Fountain when its power grows too great to be contained.
> 📜 Prophesied in the Legend of the Delta Rune as the cause of "the Roaring": the event that would devastate both the Light World and the Dark World.
> 🗣️ First mentioned in Ch.2, when Ralsei explains what the Roaring consists of — long before any is seen on screen.
> 👹 The first one to physically appear is created by the Roaring Knight at the end of Ch.4, opening a Dark Fountain much more powerful than usual (with five times more "leaves" than normal) inside the 3rd Sanctuary — it's the chapter's final boss, though not its main antagonist (that role belongs to the Knight himself).
> 🥚 As soon as it's created, the Fountain solidifies into a stone column that cracks like an egg, revealing the Titan's arm before the rest of its body.
> 🛡️ It has a Save Point-shaped shield covering its face — it can only really be damaged once the shield breaks; in the meantime, it closes up and regenerates by absorbing its own Titan Spawn.
> 👶 Its offspring, the Titan Spawn, are embryo-shaped creatures that first appear as obstacles around the Sanctuary and later as miniboss pairs, right before reaching the Titan.
> 🔨 When the battle seems impossible to win, Gerson Boom (the Old Man) appears and helps break the shield one last time with the Dual Buster — Susie then throws Kris inside the Titan itself, and Kris's SOUL shines from within and destroys it, in a gesture almost identical to sealing a Dark Fountain.
> 🚫 It doesn't speak and doesn't appear to have its own personality or cognition, according to Ralsei's own description.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> It's not a character in the usual sense — Ralsei is explicit that the Titan lacks cognition or personality. Its only recognizable "instinct" is defensive: it pulls its head behind its wings to absorb its Titan Spawn as soon as it takes damage, showing no attachment to them beyond using them as a resource — it even destroys them outright with its own attacks as soon as the shield breaks.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Would each Dark World have its own distinct Titan?
> The official wiki itself suggests that the Ch.4 Titan's form might be unique to that particular Sanctuary, and that other Titans — if any exist in other Dark Worlds — could take on completely different forms depending on the world that generates them. Unconfirmed, since only one has been seen so far.

> [!question]- Can Titan Spawn become Titans?
> It's unclear whether the Titan Spawn are actual offspring of the Titan that could grow into one, or whether they're a completely different species — the Ch.4 Titan itself seems to form almost instantly as soon as the Fountain is created, without going through any visible intermediate stage. **Fan theory, unconfirmed.**

> [!question]- 🎲 The two figures flanking the Titan in the prophetic illustration
> In the Ultimate Prophecy's drawing depicting the Roaring (at the very top of this note), the central Titan appears flanked by two identical, symmetrical silhouettes, with a spiked crown on their heads and angular wing-shaped arms — a design that strikingly matches the Roaring Knight's own official silhouette. It's a visual reading, not confirmed by any text in the game, but the resemblance is hard to overlook: compare it directly with the Knight's profile portrait.
>
> ![[TheRoaringKnightDeltarune.webp]]
> *The Roaring Knight's official silhouette — the same spiked crown and the same wing-shaped arms as the two figures flanking the Titan in the Prophecy.*
>
> If this reading is correct, the illustration could be visually foreshadowing that the Knight himself is the one who "escorts" or even generates the Titan — consistent with the fact that he is indeed the one who opens the Fountain that creates it in Ch.4 — or it could feed the theory, still with no solid textual basis, that there's more than one Knight, or that "the Roaring Knight" is more of a role/suit of armor than a single identity. Pure visual speculation for now.

## Trivia
- Its battle theme is called "GUARDIAN," a name that doesn't quite match Ralsei's description of the Titans — it could refer to the Titan "guarding" the Fountain, or be a nod to EarthBound's Sanctuary Guardians, given that Ch.4's own Dark World is called "Sanctuary" in the soundtrack.
- Like all Deltarune bosses, it has a dead pixel hidden in one of its wings — part of the recurring joke that every boss in the game includes one.
- The Titan Spawn visually resemble a young Everyman, and several fan theories link them to Giygas from EarthBound and to the Secretaries from OFF, two of Undertale and Deltarune's major declared influences.
- It's the second final boss of a chapter that isn't its main antagonist — the first was the Roaring Knight himself, defeated before Tenna in Ch.3.
