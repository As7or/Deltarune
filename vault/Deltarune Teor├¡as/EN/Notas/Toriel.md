*Table note: she has no confirmed Darkner form of her own — the Ch.3 capsule keeps her as a locked-up Lightner, it doesn't transform her. She does have her own talksprite (see Personality profile, below).*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🍎 Hometown school teacher, mother of Kris and Asriel, Asgore's ex-wife.
> 🚗 Present since Ch.1: wakes Kris up every morning and drives them to school as usual.
> 🏠 Lives with her children in a well-kept house north of town.
> 🥧 Rudy remembers her as much wilder and more energetic in college — she once hit him with a key lime pie at a party.
> 🔒 In Ch.3, Tenna secretly keeps her locked inside a gachapon capsule in the Dark World, calling her his [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "card up his sleeve."
> 👀 She's somewhat negligent with Kris without meaning to — she doesn't notice, for example, that [[Susie]] was bullying them, something Noelle comments on with surprise.
> 🍷 In Ch.4, after canceling choir practice because of the rain, she runs into Sans at the store and ends up inviting him home — Kris and Susie find her dancing the jitterbug with him, already tipsy, when they get back at night.
> 🥴 In Ch.5, she wakes up hungover from that same night, asleep on "Chairiel."

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> She keeps the good nature of her Undertale version: warm, protective, and caring by vocation. But she retains some not-so-idealized flaws — she's somewhat negligent without meaning to and occasionally quick to judge. The contrast between the present-day Toriel, calm and a bit absent-minded, and the one Rudy describes from her youth (much wilder and more energetic) is one of the most-discussed characterization details among fans.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The goodbye at school
> She wakes Kris up in the morning and drives him to school in her van as usual — they're running late — mentioning along the way that Asriel plans to visit them "next week." She says goodbye to Kris with a hug at the entrance before each of them heads to class.
>
> At night, if Kris hasn't called her first, she's the one who calls: she sounds worried but is relieved to learn Kris has been spending time with "a friend," and lets him stay out a bit later than usual.

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — The eggs with Sans, Alphys's classroom, and the cake
> At Sans's shop, Toriel comes in looking for the eggs she'd left behind, and the two trade a long round of egg jokes — until Asgore bursts into the shop joking that he's her "eggs-husband." After an awkward silence, Asgore asks her what they'll do when Asriel comes home; Toriel replies that they should talk about it together soon, before leaving the shop.
>
> Later, if Kris approaches the school hallway with the Ch.2 "trash ball," he can overhear Toriel asking Alphys whether Kris has been "doing anything weird" — Alphys replies that he's one of her favorite students and, noticing the trash ball he's wearing on his head, settles the matter by saying it's "normal." Toriel calms down a bit, though she admits to being "a little worried lately." In another conversation, she asks Kris not to let Susie's habit of eating chalk "rub off on them."
>
> In the chapter's epilogue, if Kris and Susie visit the Dreemurr house, Toriel welcomes them in and invites Susie to stay for cake.

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — Locked away by Tenna
> During the TV World, Tenna keeps secret calls with subordinates about something he wants to hide from the group. Susie grows suspicious and, investigating, they discover he'd been keeping Toriel locked in a gachapon capsule, calling her his [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "card up his sleeve" for being the one who cared about him the most. At the end of the chapter, when the Roaring Knight tries to kidnap her while she sleeps, Susie steps in to protect her, buying time until Undyne arrives to intervene.
>

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — The jitterbug with Sans
> Toriel invites Susie to go to church with Kris, lending her some of Asriel's old clothes for the occasion. There, she sings during the service along with the rest of the congregation.
>
> ![[Toriel_sing_in_the_church.png]]
> *Toriel singing during the church service, in Ch.4.*
>
> Afterward, she has some fruit juice and chats with Rudy and Noelle — if Kris approaches, the topic of Carol funding Asgore for an unspecified hobby comes up, which doesn't sit well with Toriel and cuts the conversation short; she also stops Rudy when he starts reminiscing out loud about their partying days together in college. Before heading back to church for choir practice, Asgore approaches to offer her another bouquet of flowers, which she declines, walking away uncomfortably (see her relationship with Asgore, below).
>
> With the rain, she cancels practice and takes shelter at Sans's shop, unaware that Kris and Susie are looking for her in case the Knight had kidnapped her again. Once it clears up, she invites him to her house — she's having such a good time with him that the conversation runs long — and ends up inviting him to stay. When Kris and Susie get back at night, they find her dancing the jitterbug with him, already a glass of wine too many in — she introduces him to Sans amid laughter and offers to let Susie sleep in Asriel's old bed, which Susie declines, visibly uncomfortable, before leaving.
>
> At one point in the chapter, her horns can be seen peeking above the dialogue box during a phone call.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Asgore
> Ex-husband and ex-wife; they divorced before the game begins, though they still coordinate raising Kris and Asriel. According to Asgore's monologue in Ch.5, her self-blame over an undisclosed tragedy ended up driving her away from him. In Ch.4, Asgore approaches to offer her another bouquet of flowers; she declines the gesture and walks away uncomfortably, making it clear their relationship is still tense despite the time that's passed. ## Theories

> [!question]- Why did Tenna choose her?
> Tenna describes her as the one who cared about him the most, suggesting a prior relationship (real, or only perceived by Tenna?) between them before Ch.3 — it's never fully explained why he picks her specifically as his "card up his sleeve" instead of any other Lightner.

## Trivia
- Toriel is also a character shared with Undertale, though — as with the rest of the recurring cast — Deltarune doesn't confirm the two games share a single timeline; see [[Conexión Undertale]].
- The contrast between her current personality and the one Rudy describes from her youth (much more energetic) is one of the most-cited characterization details of the adult cast.
- The jitterbug scene with Sans in Ch.4 split the community quite a bit: for some it's just another Toby Fox joke, for others it's uncomfortable for showing Kris's mother tipsy right when her son needed her most.
