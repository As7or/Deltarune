*Table note: Onionsan has no confirmed Darkner form or talksprite of his own — he only appears as a Light World NPC, by the Lake.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🧅 Recurring NPC by Hometown's Lake, with a name inherited directly from Undertale, where he lived in Waterfall.
> 🤝 Can be talked to in Ch.1 and 2, always promising to meet Kris "tomorrow" to tell them something he "can only tell a friend."
> 🎵 Mentions hearing, at night, "a little bit of a song" that seems to come from deep underwater — and in Ch.2 confirms he's heard the whole thing: "it's not a new song."
> 👻 Disappears starting in Ch.4, coinciding with Normal NPC's first appearance at his same meeting spot.
> 🎸 In Undertale, he lived alone and friendless in Waterfall after his friends moved to an overcrowded aquarium — he dreamed of one day living in the open sea.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Lonely, somewhat forgetful (he even says he forgot his own name because no one talks to him) and desperate to make friends — he asks to be given a name and clings to any conversation. Despite his loneliness, he keeps a persistent optimism about his future, just as in Undertale he believed he'd someday get to live in the ocean.

## Relationships

## Theories

> [!question]+ What was the song from the sea?
> Onion's exact line in Ch.2 — [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "I've heard that song before... coming from the sea. The whole thing. It's not a 'new' song" — is one of the most analyzed lines in the whole game. It's been theorized that it could be related to the song "Don't Forget" (which plays over the Ch.1 credits) and to [[Susie]], though none of this is confirmed; it's one of the fandom's most speculative connections.

> [!question]- What really happened to him?
> A speculative and rather macabre theory: that Normal NPC killed him to stop him from investigating that same song, and afterward sold his remains as food (takoyaki) at the Ch.5 Festival, where Susie comments it tastes [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "like raw onion." It's a fan theory with no direct textual support beyond that coincidence — more of a dark fandom joke than a serious reading of the plot.

## Trivia
- In Undertale, Onionsan is a lonely aquatic monster from Waterfall who dreams of one day living in the open sea — a wish Deltarune seems to inherit almost literally in its own version of the character.
- His internal name in the game's files is simply "onionsan," a direct holdover from his name in Undertale.
- In Undertale, calling Papyrus twice on the phone while with Onionsan makes him say "HUM HUM HUM..." — a reference to the video game studio Onion Games, a friend of Toby Fox's.
