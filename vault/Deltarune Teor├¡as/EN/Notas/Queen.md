> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 💻 Full name: Serial Number Q5U4EX7YY2E9N. She is the tyrannical ruler of the Cyber World and the main antagonist of Ch.2.
> 👑 Wants to expand her domain across the entire Light World by covering it in darkness, convinced this would make both Lightners and Darkners happy.
> 📡 After the internet blackout, she became erratic and tyrannical: she turned the Plugboys into Werewires through brainwashing and controls the media, forcing them to praise her and banning any criticism.
> 🤖 Her final combat form is the GIGA Queen mech, which she pilots after kidnapping Kris, [[Susie]], and Ralsei in her Mansion.
> 🎭 Returns after the Ch.5 credits, helping Susie decorate Castle Town for Noelle's arrival — and with no Roaring Knight in the background, she gives up on trying to stop Kris.
> 🎙️ Voiced by Toby Fox, just like Jevil, [[Flowery]], Spamton, and Tenna.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Authoritarian, ambitious, and convinced that total control is the only path to everyone's happiness — a computer's logic that treats obedience as synonymous with well-being. Despite the tyrannical facade, she shows some genuine affection toward the King (whom she nicknames "Kingy Wingy") and, after Ch.5, shows herself willing to give up and live peacefully in Castle Town. She drops punctuation erratically, uses internet acronyms like "LMAO" (which even appears literally in one of her portraits), and in the Japanese version all her dialogue is enclosed in HTML comments (`<!-- -->`), with "LMAO" replaced by "www" and "草" — the usual laughter slang of Japanese internet culture.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — The tyrant of the Cyber World
> After witnessing the Roaring Knight create a Dark Fountain, Queen becomes obsessed with creating her own. She kidnaps Noelle the moment she enters the Cyber World with Berdly, trying to turn her into a "willing pawn" that would let her expand her power — and repeats several times her wish to turn Noelle's face into a robotic one.

Berdly joins the heroes to try to rescue her, but his plan fails and both are recaptured; Queen wires a cable into his face to fully control him and sends him to attack the party.

## Relationships

> [!prophecy]- Prophecy
> ![[18_the_queens_chariot_cannot_be_stopped.gif]]
> Pane 18 ("The Queen's chariot cannot be stopped") fits thematically with her GIGA Queen from Ch.2 — an unstoppable mechanical "chariot." **Reasoned reading, no direct textual confirmation.** [[Profecía|Prophecy]]

> [!quote]- Berdly
> Her ally (and victim) in Ch.2 — together they try to build a "Smartopia," until she loses control over him and ends up forcing him into submission with a cable in his face. [[Berdly]]

> [!quote]- Noelle
> She kidnaps her the moment she arrives in the Cyber World in Ch.2, trying to turn her into her "willing pawn" to expand her own power within the system — without much concern for the effect this has on Noelle herself. She's one of the most direct and personal dangers the party faces in the whole chapter. [[Noelle]]

> [!quote]- Roaring Knight
> Her obsession with creating her own Dark Fountain comes from watching him create his — he is, in a sense, her role model until Ralsei explains to her what Roaring actually involves. [[Roaring Knight]]

> [!quote]- King
> They fondly nickname each other "Queenie Beanie" and "Kingy Wingy" upon reuniting in Castle Town — one of the few genuinely affectionate bonds Queen shows — and he gives her advice on raising Lancer. In Ch.4, the two of them confirm to the party, at the King's cell, that they were together and that Lancer is their son. According to Ramb, as kids Queen was the laptop that Kris and "a girl" used to play doctor with, with the King on Kris's side and Queen on hers — the only known detail about their shared history before the game. [[King]]

> [!quote]- Rouxls Kaard
> He doesn't seem to know her personally beyond one joke: he tells the King he's sorry that Queen has "a new man," leaving both Queen and the King completely baffled about who he means. [[Rouxls Kaard]]

> [!quote]- Lancer
> Lancer seems to admire her: he even decorates his own room in Ch.4 with Queen-themed keepsakes. Already in Castle Town, they can be found playing basketball together, and Queen also appears in his room teasing Ralsei for not having "any Meat or Fruit on the walls of his Castle." [[Lancer]]

## Theories

> [!question]- Does her cruelty come from the Dark Fountain?
> It's suggested, without conclusive proof, that the Dark Fountain itself may have intensified Queen's negative, aggressive traits — without it being clear whether this excuses her actions or simply worsens them. The game itself leaves the question open.

## Trivia
- Her full name, the serial number Q5U4EX7YY2E9N, contains, in order, all the letters needed to spell "Queen."
- GIGA Queen's battle theme, "Knock You Down!!," is inspired by the final boss theme from the SNES Mighty Morphin Power Rangers game.
- The entire battle against GIGA Queen, as well as the arcade minigame at the start of Ch.2, is a direct reference to Punch-Out!!.
