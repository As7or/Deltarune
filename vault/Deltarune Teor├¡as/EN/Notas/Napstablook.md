*Table note: Napstablook has no confirmed Darkner form of his own — he always appears as a Lightner, in his role as police officer.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 👮 Hometown police officer alongside Undyne — a post he got "by accident" and doesn't dare quit because his cousins depend on him.
> 👻 A returning character from Undertale; lives at the Blook House together with his cousins Mad Mew Mew (Pink) and Nobody (probably Mettaton).
> 📓 Mistakenly returned his own diary to the library instead of the book he had borrowed — so now he has to check it out every time he wants to write in it.
> 🐌 In Ch.4 he's shown being run over (harmlessly) again and again by the Snail Guy while trying to direct traffic without Undyne — the Snail himself admits that, with no police watching, he can finally go "as slow as he wants."
> 🎪 At the Ch.5 Festival he runs the strength tester in Undyne's place, helped by the Wet-Nose Bandits to set up the machine, and by his cousins, whom he pays with chopped-up hot dogs.
> ✋ Ch.5 reveals, for the first time, that Napstablook has hands.
> 🎧 He loves making remixes of his own songs; the jailbreak alarm that plays at the police station after the Wet-Nose Bandits escape is, in fact, a remix of his own tape.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Shy, apathetic, and low-spirited, same as in Undertale — his catchphrase ([TODO: verify exact EN quote] "the police aren't in a very good mood right now") is a direct reference to one of his harmless attacks from the original game. Despite that surface apathy, he shows genuine loyalty to his cousins and sincere concern for Undyne when she disappears.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — Behind the closed door
> He doesn't appear in person, but if you knock on the police station door, he closes the blinds from inside — the narration describes it as the police "not being in a very good mood right now."

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — Undyne's partner
> He appears in person at the end of the chapter, at the police station alongside Undyne. He explains why he can't quit the job (his cousins depend on him) and warns Kris not to do anything illegal.

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Worried about Undyne
> He's found being run over harmlessly by the Snail while trying to direct traffic alone. He mentions no one has seen Undyne since the night before, and that the Shelter code was "one of the few things that ever made her nervous."
>

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Undyne
> His work partner at the Hometown police station, where Napstablook serves as an officer. He genuinely worries about her when she disappears after being kidnapped by the Roaring Knight at the end of Ch.3, in one of the few moments where he shows such a direct emotion toward another character. [[Undyne]]

> [!quote]- Mad Mew Mew (Pink)
> His cousin, with whom he lives at the Blook House along with Nobody — the same extended family that also got trapped in a tree together with Catti's in an earlier rescue. It's one of the few confirmed family connections outside the Dreemurr/Holiday core. [[Mad Mew Mew (Pink)]]

## Trivia
- His diary, mistakenly returned to the library, can be found and read like any other book in the collection.
- The police station wall shows photos of Undyne rescuing Catti's family from a tree, with Napstablook also trapped in the same tree on that occasion — alongside an article noting that Undyne became police chief after Asgore's removal.
- The jailbreak alarm that plays after the Wet-Nose Bandits escape is a remix Napstablook made of his own tape — consistent with his already-established love, from Undertale, of remixing his own songs.
