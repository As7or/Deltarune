*Table note: Shuttah is a native Darkner, with no Light World form or confirmed talksprite of his own.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 📸 Miniboss of the Island Board (Chapter 3): a face with a camera-film-roll mouth and eyes at the end of each hand, with vertical green/blue/yellow/red/gray stripes.
> 🎞️ His Check textually confirms: *"When its eyes close, gray memories are made."* [TODO: verify exact EN quote] — his photos are, by design, black and white, even though they appear in color on screen during the battle.
> 🗣️ Extremely theatrical and affected, calls everyone "darling" and cares above all about framing and lighting.
> 🕰️ Nostalgic: he's one of the few Darkners who remembers what the TV World was like before the Dark Fountain existed.
> 🎭 He appears behind a curtain in a room next to the Green Room — visually very similar to Undertale's True Lab — before becoming a common enemy after Board 3.
> 🐙 He shares a design with Krumm, from the series *Aaahh!!! Real Monsters* (a big mouth with no head, eyes on the hands).

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Flamboyant, flirty, and a bit world-weary — he declares himself bored with his own photos ("usually, all my photos are gray..." [TODO: verify exact EN quote]) until the color the main party brings back restores his enthusiasm. He's kind despite how strange his battle is, and shows no real hostility toward Kris, Susie, or Ralsei.
>

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — the Island Board
> After getting the Camera (which can only take black-and-white photos within this board) and collecting enough Great Photos, the group reaches a room with two pillars and a table that, once the rest of the board is completed, take on the shape of Shuttah's eyes and mouth — the set itself "becomes" him before the battle. Before the battle starts, he asks the group for a photo of the three of them together; once he gets it, he laughingly accuses them of being "criminals" for "stealing" the sun, the moon, and the clouds with their photos, as well as the camera itself — a "mugshot" of the three thieves.
>
> ![[shuttah_taking_photo_retro_game_black_and_white.webp]]
> *The photo minigame against Shuttah, in black and white within the Island Board.*
>
> During the battle itself, each ACT challenges you to photograph a different subject (any character, Shuttah's own smile, Lancer's tongue, or the whole trio) — photographing Ralsei full-body, or leaving him as the sole subject of the final shot, gets him excited comments about how well those photos would sell.
>
> ![[shuttah_black_and_white.jpg]]
> *One of the resulting photos, in black and white — the style Shuttah associates with his own memories.*
>
> After being defeated/spared, Shuttah admits that his photos "are usually all gray," but that the group has given him back "the sky, blue, blue, blue" [TODO: verify exact EN quote] — his recruit type becomes, fittingly, "Copy."
>
> ![[Shuttah_battle_spared.gif]]
> *Shuttah right after being spared, already acknowledging the group gave him back his color.*
>
> ![[Shuttah_overworld_hand.gif]]
> *One of Shuttah's eyes, located at the end of his hand.*
>
> He reappears later as an NPC in the Green Room hallway, speaking nostalgically about the time when the TV World didn't yet have a Dark Fountain, and mentions knowing about the existence of the Forgotten Island and the old hermit himself (the Forgotten Man) — one of the few Darkners who shows that level of knowledge about the world before Tenna.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Kris
> He's the one who uses the Camera throughout the board — Shuttah goes as far as accusing the group of being "criminals" for "stealing" the sun, the moon, and the clouds with their photos, before flirtatiously asking for a photo of himself/herself too. [[Kris]]

His "gray memories" theme also connects to the same semantic field of gray/monochrome/photography surrounding the other characters linked to him (the Followers, the Goners). [[Gaster (W. D. ## Theories

> [!question]- Why are his photos gray instead of colored?
> Fan reading (not fully confirmed): the fact that Shuttah can only capture "gray" memories until the main party shows up fits the same color symbolism surrounding Kris — see the envy-of-Asriel theory in [[Kris]] — and the same "monochrome = copy/absence of identity" pattern already used by Gaster's Followers and the Vessel. There's no textual confirmation that the two things are connected beyond sharing the same thematic field.

## Trivia
- His internal development name was "Camenary," visible in cut dialogue (an unused ACT that photographed Susie mid-sneeze) and in an unused white silhouette sprite in the final version.
- Before the third show, if you leave the Green Room, he has a line mentioning "purple cliffs," "a frozen wasteland," and "a metal desert" [TODO: verify exact EN quote] — coyly naming the same iconic Undertale zones (Waterfall, Snowdin, Hotland).
- Getting a Z rank in his battle cuts off access to the rest of that round's minigames, leaving only a small dark room with glitched posters and Spamton's old dressing room — one of the most direct ways the game "punishes" someone deliberately playing badly.
