> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🏛️ Mayor of Hometown, Rudy's wife, mother of Dess and Noelle.
> 😐 Efficient and not very charismatic in public, but according to Asgore much more pleasant once you get to know her.
> 🚧 Personally investigates the southern part of town, blocked off by flower boxes — implicitly the Shelter.
> ⚔️ She keeps two katanas at home: one in the kitchen, with which she manages to cut a door-to-door salesman's briefcase in two, and another —black— in her own bedroom.
> 🛏️ Dess's room remains untouched in her house; it's implied she deeply misses her even though it's hard for her to show it openly.
> 💥 She met Rudy when, the moment he saw her, he told her she was *"a HOT piece of work"* — she responded by slapping him unconscious, and they still ended up getting married.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Strict, controlling, and utterly focused on appearances — especially with Noelle, of whom she demands far more than is reasonable. Hostile toward [[Susie]], whom she sees as a bad influence, but surprisingly hospitable toward Kris. Behind the cold-efficiency facade is someone who obsessively preserves everything related to Dess, unable to openly process her loss. She was elected mayor for her competence, not her charisma — of which she has almost none.
>

## History by chapter

> [!example]+ Chapters 1 and 2 — the absence you notice
> Although she's mentioned starting in Ch.1, Carol doesn't appear in person until Ch.4 — her physical absence during the early chapters already hints at a disconnect between the family and the town's social life, coinciding with Dess's disappearance.

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — return to Noelle's house
> She makes her first in-person appearance, coming home abruptly to Noelle's house right after Kris makes a mysterious call from the closet. She finds Susie playing Dess's guitar for Noelle, confiscates the instrument, inspects it, and orders Noelle to the kitchen — making clear how protective (and controlling) she is of anything related to her missing daughter.
> ![[sussie_with_carol.png]]
*Carol confronting Susie right after finding her with Dess's guitar.*
>
>Despite the tension of the moment, she apologizes afterward to Kris for the rough experience and makes clear he'll always be welcome in her house — a notably different treatment from the one she gives Susie, whom she throws out without hesitation.
>
> In a flashback in this chapter (during Tenna's storyline), the Holiday and Dreemurr families are seen watching TV together at the Dreemurrs' house — until, one by one, they start vanishing from the scene. Carol is the second to fade away, right after Dess.
>

> > On this route, Kris never makes the mysterious call before Susie finds the guitar — she finds out on her own, after leaving the house. The message Carol leaves at the end of this route —confirming Kris's attendance at the Festival and mentioning that Noelle can't wait to see him— sounds like her normal, recognizable voice, unlike the strange voice-distorter used in the mysterious call on the normal route.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Rudy
> Her husband. They met when he told her she was *"a HOT piece of work"* — she knocked him out cold with a slap, and they still got married and had Dess and Noelle. The anecdote is never shown on screen: Kris overhears it at the Hospital, when Rudy tells it to Noelle while she's visiting (accessible as early as Ch.1-2). Despite the abruptness of how they met, they come across as a solid, affectionate couple day to day, even though the game barely shows them together on screen. Before Dess disappeared, the two of them used to frequent the Dreemurr house on special dates —something they stopped doing afterward— which suggests their daughter's loss also hit their social life as a couple, not just her relationship with Noelle. [[Rudy]]
>
> ![[rudy_talks_about_carol.png]]

> [!quote]- Toriel
> Families who've been friends for years, but the one relationship between the two families that hasn't mended over time — in Ch.4, Toriel coldly corrects Rudy when he calls Carol "his wife" in front of her, and only admits to being "grateful" for her financial help to Asgore, with no real enthusiasm. [[Toriel]]

> [!quote]- Dess
> Her eldest daughter, missing before the events of the game. She keeps her room untouched, and her insistence on preserving everything important reads as a way of not fully letting go — she reacts with disproportionate harshness on seeing Susie playing Dess's guitar, as if any contact with her things were a threat to the little that's left of her. [[Dess]]
>
> ![[The_Dreemurrs_and_Holiday_Families.webp]]
> *The flashback of the Holiday and Dreemurr families gathered together — Dess among them.*

> [!quote]- Noelle
> Her other daughter. Their relationship is marked by the pressure of keeping up appearances after Dess's disappearance — Carol channels into Noelle the same fear that Dess's absence left her with, demanding exemplary performance and behavior to the point that Noelle lives in constant anxiety about disappointing her. Both relationships —the idealized absence of Dess and the suffocating pressure on Noelle— seem to be two sides of the same unresolved loss. [[Noelle]]

> [!quote]- Asgore
> She's his landlord: Asgore owes rent on the Flower King florist shop, which she generously lets slide. They also share an almost identical documents corkboard, suggesting both are investigating something in parallel. [[Asgore]]

> [!quote]- Kris
> Surprisingly warm toward him compared to Susie — she lets him into her house without issue and apologizes personally if things get tense. If the mysterious call in Ch.4 were really hers, it would make her the person Kris talks to most in secret during the chapter. [[Kris]]

> [!quote]- Roaring Knight
> As mayor, she has legitimate access to practically any key or code in town — and yet she seems to deliberately cover up or ignore several clues related to the Roaring Knight, which feeds the (unconfirmed) theory that she knows more than she lets on. Her personal investigation of the Shelter area, exactly where the Knight took refuge with Undyne, only reinforces that suspicion. [[Roaring Knight]]

## Theories

> [!question]+ Is Carol the Roaring Knight?
> ![[Carol_face_ominous_unused.png|160]]
> *A discarded portrait of Carol with a far more unsettling expression than usual — part of the fandom points to it as a possible design clue behind the theory.*
>
> The so-called "Carol-Knight" or "Mayor Knight" is one of the fandom's most long-standing theories. In favor: the Knight's antlers resemble Carol's more than Dess's (more pointed, not rounded); she has direct access to the shelter through her position; she owns two katanas, one of them black, very similar to the Knight's Black Knife; and the briefcase salesman mentions she "killed" his briefcase with a giant katana, a verb that fits the Knight's weapon.
>
> Against: she's seen at her own house at the exact moment the group faces the Knight at the church, which —barring teleportation— makes it impossible for her to be the same person at that specific instant; her body proportions differ quite a bit from the Knight's; the Knight has a distorted, ghostly appearance tied to Gaster that Carol never shows; and her movements are stiff and serious, very unlike the Knight's, which floats in a fluid, unsettling way. The Black Knife, moreover, is wielded one-handed, while a katana like hers is normally handled with two. **Fan theory, with strong points but also serious objections — unconfirmed.**
>
> ![[dess_knight3.png]]

> [!question]- Does she collaborate with the Knight without being him/her?
> If the previous theory doesn't quite add up, an alternative reading —and one more widespread among those who reject that Carol literally is the Knight— is that she consciously collaborates with or covers for him: hiding clues, protecting information about the shelter, and acting with a familiarity toward the phenomenon that an ordinary mayor wouldn't have. Under this reading, her suspicious actions would still point to complicity without her needing to be the Knight's physical entity herself. **Fan theory, unconfirmed.**

> [!question]- Does she collaborate with Kris? Is she the mysterious caller?
> Two distinct calls in Ch.4 need to be told apart, which the fandom often mixes up:
> 1. **The vent call**: while Kris's SOUL travels through the vents of Noelle's house, it finds Kris already on the phone with someone unidentified, asking them to keep Susie from finding Dess's guitar. Carol arrives at the door seconds after that call ends — that timing coincidence is what most fuels the suspicion that it's her. This call, though, knows very specific things: that the church Fountain will open that night, that "the police officer" (almost certainly Undyne) will be sacrificed the following week, and it specifically asks that Susie not see the shelter code — motivations that fit both the Knight and Carol herself.
> 2. **The window call**: later that night, at the end of the chapter, Kris gets a second, different call that only reminds him of a promise ("Kris... don't forget... you promised"). This is the one most often cited as a possible clue to the Knight himself, and the one mistakenly conflated with the first.
>
> There's also a **third call, this one practically confirmed as hers**: after the chapter's Fountains are sealed, Kris receives a voicemail that literally uses Carol's voice sample (though without her portrait in the dialogue box), confirming that Noelle can't wait to see him at the Festival the next day — the same welcoming line she says in person further below, in the Angel theory. This one is practically confirmed as her because of the actual sound used, unlike the other two. **Overall: the vent call and the window call remain unconfirmed; the Festival one, by contrast, does use Carol's real sound.**

> [!question]- Does she know about the Angel (the player)?
> The strongest piece doesn't come from any phone call, but from an in-person scene with no ambiguity about who's speaking: at her own house, next to the family display case/altar, Carol tells Kris "As you know, YOU are welcome here any time" — with the word "YOU" in red capitals, the exact same typographic style the mysterious call in Ch.4 uses to mark when it's addressing the Player rather than Kris. Unlike the call (see previous theory, with two distinct, unconfirmed speakers), this line is unquestionably hers. If that reading is correct, Carol would be one of the few characters —alongside the Voice, Ralsei, and, under certain readings, Sans— with some degree of awareness that someone is controlling Kris from outside.
>
> ![[Carol_you_are_welcome.png]]
> *"As you know, YOU are welcome here any time." — Carol, in person, using the same typographic style the mysterious call uses to address the Player.*
>
> This, combined with the mysterious call also using that same typographic mark, is what feeds the idea that —whether or not she's the one calling— Carol does seem to at least suspect the existence of the Player as an entity separate from Kris. **Fan theory; the in-person scene with "YOU" is a confirmed fact, the interpretation that it implies awareness of the Player is still interpretation.**

> [!question]- The black katana in her bedroom
> Besides the kitchen katana (used to cut the salesman's briefcase), Carol keeps a second katana, this one black, in her own bedroom — very similar in appearance to the Black Knife wielded by the Knight. It's, along with the dark crystal fragments Asgore mentions keeping "to improve his life," one of the most-cited environmental clues linking the Holiday family (or Asgore) to the Knight's weapon. **Fan theory, with no direct confirmation of what that object actually is.**
>
> ![[Carol_and_Rudy_room.png]]
> *Carol and Rudy's bedroom — the black katana, barely visible in the shadows, is one of the most-analyzed environmental details in the whole house.*

> [!question]- Why does she investigate the southern area and hide the shelter codes?
> Carol personally investigates the southern part of town, blocked by flower boxes — almost certainly the Shelter. As mayor, she'd have legitimate access to both the police code and the church code, even without needing any complicity with the Knight; she herself is the one who controls the code for the town's Christmas tree, for instance. The mysterious call in Ch.4 specifically asks that Susie not get to see the code stored in Dess's guitar — if that call is Carol, hiding the code would stop being mere administrative caution and become an active attempt to keep the group from accessing the shelter. The simplest reading is that, whether or not she knows exactly what's inside, Carol suspects the shelter is connected to Dess's disappearance and would rather investigate it alone than let Susie or Kris get into something dangerous. **A combination of confirmed fact (she investigates the area) and fan theory (the exact reason she hides the code).**
>
> ![[carol_investigation_shelter.webp]]

> [!question]- Does she work with Asgore for more than just the florist shop's rent?
> ![[Asgore_y_Carol_corcho.webp]]
> *Carol and Asgore*
>
> Carol is Asgore's landlord —she rents him the Flower King space with more generosity than you'd expect from someone so uncharismatic— but the two of them also share an almost identical investigation corkboard, visible in both their houses. Asgore, for his part, mentions at one point when alone that he's planning to "improve his life" with a fragment of dark crystal, which implies he needs someone to teach him how to use it. On top of this, both lost someone close to them tied to the Dark Worlds —Carol lost Dess, Asgore possibly his own son Asriel, who left shortly after all this— which gives rise to the reading that their collaboration goes beyond real estate: they could be investigating the Dark Fountains phenomenon together, in parallel or directly coordinated. **Fan theory, supported by several converging environmental clues; unconfirmed.**

## Trivia

- Her facial features visually recall the Grinch from certain film adaptations of "How the Grinch Stole Christmas."
- Before Dess disappeared, Carol and Rudy used to visit the Dreemurr house often for holidays — something they stopped doing shortly after.
- She has a fondness for heart-shaped objects, with a collection of heart-shaped pillows according to Noelle.
- She's capable of splitting a door-to-door salesman's briefcase in two with a single stroke — a rather impressive show of strength and katana skill for someone who, on the surface, is just a small-town mayor.
- There's an unused variant of her dialogue portrait, with a considerably more sinister expression than the one actually used in the game — cut content the fandom often cites given how well it fits the theories about what Carol is hiding.
