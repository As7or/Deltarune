> [!info]+ Quick facts
> - 🦌 **Species:** reindeer monster — short fur, red nose, two antlers, floppy ears
> - 🏠 **World:** Lightner (Hometown)
> - 👪 **Family:** Holiday — father Rudy, mother Carol (mayor), older sister Dess
> - ⚔️ **Narrative role:** tritagonist; deuteragonist of the Weird Route
> - ❄️ **Battle title:** Snowcaster → Frostmancer → Ice Trancer (with Thorn Ring)
> - 🎄 **Design trivia:** "Noelle" comes from "Noël" (Christmas in French); her whole design is Christmas-themed
> - 💌 **Secretly in love** with Susie, without telling anyone except, obliquely, her father

## Who she is
Classmate of Kris and Susie, Kris's neighbor since childhood. Shy, kind, and prone to constantly apologizing — her shyness and constant need for approval largely reflect the pressure she gets at home, especially from Carol.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> - She's afraid of humans hiding under her bed (because of Kris's childhood pranks) — her father describes her as someone who "gets scared of everything" and flatly "helpless."
> - She may have slightly masochistic traits: she fantasizes about Susie "scolding" her and says the Ragger weapon "feels good."
> - She loves horror movies even though they scare her.
> - She has a personal blog ("holidaygirl1225") dedicated to "video game secrets, glitches, and theories" — its existence is confirmed within Chapter 5 itself, not only in the ARG.
> - She's left-handed, like Dess and, apparently, Carol.
> - Before Kris, [[Berdly]] secretly copied her notes to seem like the smartest kid in class.
> - As a kid, Kris made her believe ICE-E was real and ate children, until Dess hit him with a wiffle ball bat to make him stop lying to her — the same kind of weapon wielded by the Roaring Knight (see below).

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — a minor role, but a key one
> When Kris enters class, Noelle offers to let him join her project group along with Berdly — she tries to ask Alphys for permission, but Berdly interrupts, insisting they're "fine on their own." Later she offers (along with Susie) to go get chalk, though Alphys ends up sending Kris with Susie instead. If spoken to before she leaves, she tries to comfort Kris by saying she doesn't think Susie is that bad.
>
> ![[Noelle_screenshot_greeting_Kris.png]]
> *Noelle greeting Kris at the start of the chapter.*
>
> After Kris's first foray into the Dark World, she's found visiting her father Rudy in the hospital, who gives her (questionable) advice on how to confess her feelings to Susie. She's one of the only classmates who knew Kris *before* "you" started controlling them, so she finds it odd that he's suddenly talking so much, when normally they're very quiet.

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — her big starring role
> The next day she tries to read aloud in class, but Berdly interrupts her. After class, she invites Kris and Susie to study with her and Berdly in the library — but on arriving, it turns out Noelle and Berdly have already fallen into a new Dark World in the computer room. On the way there, traffic in Hometown is blocked by a dog stuck in a toy car, with Undyne trying to keep order.
>
> ![[Noelle_overworld_traffic.gif]]
> *Noelle crossing the traffic on her way to the Cyber World.*
>
> In the Cyber World, **Queen captures her** and tries to recruit her for her world-domination cause, treating her like her own daughter with affectionate nicknames ("sweetie," "honey"). She escapes and tries to hide from Queen; Kris finds her in Cyber City and briefly joins the group, helping her get over her fear of mice. She's recaptured along with Kris, Susie, and Berdly, and sent to her own room in Queen's Mansion.
>
> ![[Noelle_screenshot_Chapter_2_firstbattle.png]]
> *The first battle of Chapter 2 alongside Noelle, already in the Cyber World.*
>
> During a series of puzzles, it's revealed that Berdly secretly copied Noelle's notes to seem like the smartest kid in class — until a spelling bee got in the way for her (the word was "DECEMBER," and the emotional block cost her the win). Susie rescues her, convinces her it's all a dream, and they share a ride on the Ferris wheel up to the mansion's roof — Noelle confides in her about watching horror movies with Dess. Berdly later confesses to her that he's actually in love with Susie, not her.
>
> Queen tries to force her to become the new Knight, mistakenly believing the original Knight had disappeared. When Queen threatens Susie to force Noelle into opening a new Dark Fountain, Noelle stands her ground and refuses — Ralsei reminds them of the Prophecy, specifically the part about "The Roaring," and they decide not to create a new fountain.
>
> Apart from this chapter, while Kris's SOUL travels separately through Noelle's house for the first time in search of Dess's guitar (see below), Susie accidentally knocks over the Christmas tree and the SOUL gets trapped under the angel doll that Dess and Noelle built together as kids — Noelle, convinced it's a mouse, catches it with a frying pan before carrying it to the living room along with Susie.
>
> > [!danger]- Chapter 2 Weird Route
> > The Player, through Kris, pressures Noelle to become "stronger" by freezing enemies with IceShock instead of sparing them. She's talked into stealing the Freeze Ring from a shop, which makes her increasingly ambivalent about killing enemies, convinced it's "making her stronger." From this point on, whenever the "Proceed" option appears, it must always be chosen to avoid breaking the route; in fact, if the SOUL hasn't been returned to Kris's body in time before this, he notices its presence and bolts out of the room dragging Noelle with him, aborting the route on the spot. The route culminates when the Player forces her to confront Berdly and cast **SnowGrave**, a spell she didn't know she knew — trapping him in an ice crystal he never wakes up from (though Noelle believes he simply fell asleep). Noelle leaves the group, stunned, and doesn't reappear until finishing off Spamton NEO with a devastating IceShock.
> >
> > ![[Noelle_battle_SnowGrave.gif]]
> > *The casting of SnowGrave on Berdly — a spell Noelle herself didn't know she knew.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — absent, yet present
> She doesn't appear in person, but the White Cloak that follows the player in the second S-rank minigame is, according to the game's own code, a representation of her. She also appears in a flashback of Tenna's, at the same age she was during the spelling bee — right as Dess's disappearance was starting to be hinted at.

The player can eavesdrop on the conversation between the two girls for much of the chapter. Once they reach Dess's room, Susie ends up picking up the guitar and playing it in front of Noelle, who's uncomfortable at first about it being touched, but ends up at ease hearing it play again. Asgore reveals he was cleaning the Holiday house, and warns that Carol is about to come in — Carol, threateningly, sends Noelle to the kitchen to scold her. Before all this, at the church, she learns that her mother keeps everything related to the Shelter at home.

> > [!danger]- Chapter 4 Weird Route
> > Already at her house, Noelle asks to speak with Kris alone; he tears out his SOUL and hides it in a gift box in the guest room before entering Noelle's room. The player then guides the SOUL — now freed from Kris's body — through the air vents to that same room; if it isn't returned to the body in time, the scene cuts and the route is aborted outright.
> >
> > Inside, Noelle asks "the voice" if it can hear her, asks it to name colors to check, and then demands it say that "it didn't happen" and that "it didn't snow" that day — the player, through the responses, insists that it did happen and that it did snow. When she tells the voice it's lying, the player answers that she "will become" or "is becoming" stronger, and Noelle freezes in shock. The player then commands "Equip": Kris raises the thorn, and Noelle asks, trembling, whether what he's holding is the ThornRing.
> >
> > From here on, every choice offers two options, and one of them is always "Proceed" — if "Please Don't" is chosen instead, the route is aborted right there, and Noelle simply comments that Kris didn't say anything else. Choosing "Proceed" again and again, Kris starts crawling toward her; Noelle weakly denies what's happening, asking herself why she doesn't stop Kris. The player keeps pushing "Proceed" until Kris grabs her wrist and, with one last "Equip," forces the ThornRing onto her. The screen fades to black and a red crack grows from her hand — an animation that, at the original launch, was a rose blooming and wilting, which Toby Fox changed in an emergency patch to a simple red dot after community criticism over its reading as a metaphor for sexual assault, and days later to the final image of cracking glass. The scene cuts to Kris in the bathroom, with a high-pitched tinnitus ringing and blurred vision, picking up the SOUL, throwing it in the trash, and kicking it repeatedly.

> Before the game began, Noelle and Dess were very close: they watched scary movies together and Dess comforted her when something scared her, as well as punishing Kris when he played mean pranks on her. Dess's disappearance marked her deeply — as a child, she completely blanked when she had to spell the word "December" in a spelling bee (her sister's real name), losing the competition due to the emotional block. She still keeps several screensavers with photos of her, and avoids entering her room or touching her things out of respect or sadness, though Susie eventually convinces her to loosen those principles a little. Dess played guitar and sang in a band, "Blood Crushers The Band," very likely alongside Asriel. See [[Dess]] for her full profile.

## Theories

> [!question]+ The Angel of the Prophecy?
> During the 2022 Spamton Sweepstakes, one of the real prizes was a white wristwatch engraved with the name "Noelle Holiday"; in its description, Spamton presents it as a jewel that belonged to someone "rich and powerful," and caps off the joke with "if you need proof, there's the name of the Angel with torn wings." [TODO: verify exact EN quote] The watch matches the design of the Silver Ring that Noelle has equipped in-game — the same item needed to unlock the darkest dialogue at the end of the Snowgrave Route — which has, over the years, reinforced the theory that Noelle is the "Angel" mentioned in the Prophecy. **A real merchandising/ARG clue that supports the theory; the game itself never confirms it directly.** See the full breakdown in [[Ángel|Angel]].
>
> ![[The_Legend_screenshot_Angel%27s_Heaven.webp]]
> *The Angel's Heaven, as illustrated by the Prophecy — the figure this theory connects to Noelle.*

> [!question]+ The second hero of the Prophecy?
> The second hero's silhouette in the full Prophecy wields a sword — and although it's impossible in a normal playthrough, editing the save file can force Noelle to join the party in Chapters 3 and 4, revealing that she does have her own interactions with the team planned in advance: she can equip the Black Shard (the strongest sword in the game, normally exclusive to Kris) as well as the Jingle Blade, another sword. Ralsei also implies that being able to equip ribbons is an ability shared specifically by the three heroes of the Prophecy, and Noelle can equip the PrincessRibbon without issue. Curiously, if the Shadow Mantle is tried on her, she rejects it with the line "this is for someone... taller" [TODO: verify exact EN quote], and the description text for equipping her with the Black Shard is completely blank — a typical sign of unfinished content in the game's files, not a narrative rejection. None of this is accessible in a normal playthrough, so it counts as design data found by the community, not confirmation within the playable story.
>
> ![[12_the_second_hero_the_girl_with_hope_crossed_on_her_heart.gif]]
> *The Prophecy's stained-glass window about "the second hero": "The second hero. The girl, with hope crossed on her heart." — of the three silhouettes, she's the only one the text itself explicitly identifies as a girl.*

> [!question]- How much does she really know about "you"?
> In the Weird Route, Noelle is one of the few characters in all of Deltarune who becomes aware of the Player's influence over Kris — an awareness shared, to varying degrees, only by the Voice from the intro and Ralsei. Even in the normal route, she hints at noticing Kris acting "different from his usual self" at certain moments, without fully putting it into words. She is, along with Ralsei, one of the characters who come closest to "breaking" that fourth wall without doing so openly.
>
> ![[noelle_kris_you_changed.png]]
> *"Kris, YOU actually changed!" — by the lake, in the Chapter 5 Weird Route, directly voicing what the rest of the route only hints at.*

> [!question]- Is she herself just another "key," paralleling Yellow?
> In the Chapter 3 secret Sword Route, the White Cloak (which represents her within that minigame) is consumed in a door exactly like the Ice Key, with the two notices appearing back-to-back on screen: "The ice key was used up. She was used up." — treating her, literally, as just another key that runs out. See the full breakdown of this comparison, including the "Just-Ice" wordplay with Yellow's Yellow Key, in [[7 Flores de Colores|7 Colored Flowers]]. **A highly speculative fan theory, with no direct confirmation.**
>
> ![[noelle_she_was_used_up.png]]
> *The literal notice that accompanies the White Cloak when it's consumed at the door — treating her as just another key.*
>
> ![[noelle_key_theory_yellow_key.webp]]
> *The same kind of notice — "(The Yellow Key got used up.)" — appears with Yellow's Yellow Key, the direct basis for the "Just-Ice" parallel between the two.*

> [!question]- 🎲 If the Knight opened the Fountain, why does everything revolve around her?
> A fact that corrects a widely spread idea in the fandom: the Chapter 2 Fountain (the one in the library lab, the origin of the Cyber World) **is not opened by Noelle** — the wiki confirms it's opened by the Roaring Knight, at some point after Chapter 1. Before that confirmation, there was an analysis-video theory debating whether it had been her or, alternatively, Asriel — the argument being that much of the Cyber World revolves around the spelling bee where Noelle blanked having to spell "December" (Dess's real name) after her disappearance, something Asriel could have witnessed and she couldn't. With the Knight confirmed as the one who opens the Fountain, the question shifts: why is a world he opens so centered on such a personal, specific trauma of Noelle's? There's still no textual answer — the exact mechanic of "whose will" shapes a Dark World isn't entirely clear. See [[Fuentes Oscuras|Dark Fountains]].
>
> ![[Noelle_contest_spell.png]]
> *The spelling bee: Noelle blanks having to spell "December," Dess's real name.*
>
> 🎲 A 2026 theory (see [[Roaring Knight]]) offers a possible way out of this contradiction: if the Knight were a fusion of Noelle with [[Kris]] rather than a single person, a world opened "by the Knight" could easily revolve around Noelle's trauma without requiring that she opened it alone — both halves of the fusion would contribute their own will. It's just as speculative as the amalgam theory itself, but it fits this particular fact better than the single-person reading the Dess and Asriel theories rely on.

> [!question]- Who is "she" in "How long did it take her to smile?"
> Since the May 2025 Spamton Sweepstakes there's been a real page (deltarune.com/chapter4/thankyou/) with the exact question "How long did it take her to smile?", asking for an email and a numeric answer; every valid submission generated a blank confirmation email, followed by months of silence. In November 2025 the page reopened with the same question — this time, whoever submitted something like a reasonable answer received an email with a cryptic hint along the lines of "so, she never stopped smiling" or "so, before the sun went down," along with the phrase "Move forward with this answer." After the Chapter 5 announcement, the site opened a third time with a different question ("Where will it take place?"), apparently accessible only to those who had already received that phrase before — during that window the site itself displayed a counter reading "1,571 participants available, 1,229 responses received." The community mostly interprets "she" as Noelle, tied to her Weird Route, though some point to Dess as well — with no official confirmation of which character it refers to or what the "correct" answer is. **A real ARG puzzle, verifiable in its mechanics; the identity of "she" and the meaning of the hints remain officially unconfirmed.**

## Stat menu nickname
She doesn't appear with a title until Chapter 2, where she starts as "Snowcaster" ("Might know some cool moves"). She levels up to "Frostmancer" ("Freezes the enemy") the first time she uses the IceShock spell — earning this title is, in fact, what raises her level from 1 to 2, since Noelle is the only party member who starts directly at LV1. In the Weird Route she levels up to "Ice Trancer" ("Takes pain to grow stronger") when the ThornRing is forced onto her; the title stays even if the route is aborted afterward, unless it's replaced with "Moss Neutral" if Kris finds moss with her in the party (something that can't happen within the Weird Route itself). Her only Chapter 5 title, "Mistletoe" ("Today things got serious"), can't be reached in a normal playthrough because Noelle never appears in that chapter's Dark World — it can only be seen by editing the save file.

## Trivia
- Her blog handle "holidaygirl1225" confirms the Christmas theme down to the last detail.
- She's on the school's cross-country team, along with Jockington and (previously) Asriel.
- She can equip both the Jingle Blade and the Black Shard, both swords normally reserved for Kris.
- In a blog entry added in May 2025 to the Spamton Sweepstakes ("/rain/"), she recounts that a day of very heavy rain made Carol cancel classes, and that Catti stopped by her house to play Cat Petters. She also mentions a "weird" cat that lowered her score in the minigame, and suspects that "someone" she knows has seen it too, though she thinks they wouldn't give her a straight answer if she asked — almost certainly a reference to Kris and FRIEND (see [[FRIEND]]).
- A young Noelle can be seen next to the Snowdin Christmas tree during Undertale's 10th anniversary stream.
