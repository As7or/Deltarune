*Table note: Mr. Cattenheimer has no confirmed Darkner form or talksprite of his own — he's a purely Light World NPC.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🐈 Father of Catty and Catti, husband of Mom Cat; lives with his family in the house south of Toriel's, in Hometown.
> 🖐️ He's missing his right arm — a visible, confirmed design trait, with no explanation given in the game.
> 🔐 His house's door is a locked cat flap that, when inspected, turns out to require **3 codes** to open.
> 🍊 He's allergic to oranges (poisonous to cats) and has had to get his stomach pumped from eating them.
> ⛪ In Chapter 4 he sings in the church choir alongside Toriel, Shyren, and Noelle, dressed in religious robes.
> 🍖 He likes grilling cat food in his backyard, on a visibly broken grill.
> 👨‍👦 He mentions a brother whom Kris has nicknamed "Doug," who used to complain about his wife's cooking — a possible nod to Bud Dink, from the TV series *Doug*.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Jovial and carefree, sharing Catty's cheerful temperament. A background NPC without much direct narrative weight — but his design quietly concentrates, without any explanation in the game's own text, several of the loose pieces the fandom has long been trying to fit together about the future of Kris's hand.

## Theories

> [!question]- 🎲 The parallel between the 3 codes and the Shelter
> Both the Cattenheimer house door and the Shelter door (see [[Shelter]]) share a very specific, uncommon design within the game: a lock requiring **three independent codes** to open. It's a real, verifiable structural parallel in the actual text of both scenes, but **there's no confirmation that it's intentional** beyond the design coincidence — it could simply be reuse of the same gag ("absurdly elaborate code door for something trivial"), with no thematic link between the two doors.

> [!question]+ 🎲 The missing hand, and the warning about cats
> This is the most speculative piece, and it's best read alongside what's already documented in [[Kris]] (the "Hand theory" section) and in [[Mad Mew Mew (Pink)]]:
> - Mr. Cattenheimer is, to date, the only named Hometown NPC with a family of his own who's explicitly **missing a hand/arm** — a design detail with no textual explanation.
> - Pink's secret dialogue in Chapter 5 confirms for the first time, textually, that **a cat has bitten Kris's hand**, and that there's a "pack of hunting cats" after the protein — dialogue that the [[Mad Mew Mew (Pink)]] note itself mainly connects to [[FRIEND]] and the Eggs.
> - This theory isn't meant to replace that already-established reading (FRIEND as the origin of the hunting cats), but to point to a complementary reading: the Cattenheimer family is, literally, a family of cats living within the town, and their father is the only character who already shows on screen the physical result of "losing a hand" — the same fate the fandom has long speculated for Kris.
> - It's a connection resting on several loose design coincidences (the missing hand, the 3-code lock, being surrounded by cats) and not on any direct textual quote confirming it — it's classified as a weak theory, not a solid reading.
>
