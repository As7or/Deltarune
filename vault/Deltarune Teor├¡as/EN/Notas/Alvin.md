*Table note: Alvin has no confirmed Darkner form or talksprite of his own — he's a secondary Lightner who never enters any Dark World on screen.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> - ⛪ Parish priest of Hometown's church.
> - 🐢 Turtle monster, son of the late Gerson Boom.
> - ✍️ A frustrated writer — he didn't follow in his father's footsteps out of fear of always living in his shadow.
> - 🕯️ He keeps Gerson's dust and hammer in a jar in his office — unknowingly, this is what allows his father to be revived as a Darkner (the Old Man) in Ch.4.
> - 🙏 He firmly believes in faith, tradition, and the Prophecy; he treats the Angel with genuine religious reverence.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Kind, reflective, and easygoing — he encourages everyone to share what's on their mind with him. He has a subtle sense of humor (he fondly remembers Asriel's fake "confessions") and carries a real insecurity about not living up to his father's literary legacy.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — first appearance
> He can be found at the church doors at the end of the chapter, already established as the town's parish priest.

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — mourning at the cemetery
> He appears by his father's grave at the cemetery. He reflects on everything Gerson did in life and laments that Kris never got to have him as a teacher; he also shows concern over whether Alphys will manage to live up to the role as his replacement. He speaks fondly of Asriel, recalling the times he'd come to "confess his sins" to him, many of them committed in Kris's name. Left alone, he murmurs to himself whether his father would have been proud of him, and wonders aloud whether it's alright to do "this" with the hammer — without specifying what, in one of the earliest and most-cited hints about what he's hiding in his office.
>
> ![[alvin_in_the_grave_of_his_father.png]]
> *Alvin at his father's grave, in Hometown's cemetery.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — the sermon and the office
> He gives the Sunday sermon at the church. If asked afterward about the Shelter, he warns Kris to stay away from it. When Dark Fountains later open up inside the church itself, Alvin has already left and never appears in the Dark World. It's then that it's discovered he keeps his father's dust and hammer in a jar in his office — the reason Gerson can be temporarily revived as a Darkner in the Sanctuary. At the end of the chapter, Susie rewrites, in his name, a letter Gerson leaves for him, in which he finally tells him he was proud and encourages him to write his own story, even if it isn't perfect.
>
> ![[alvin_susie_discover_gerson_dust.webp]]
> *Susie discovering the jar with Gerson's dust and hammer in Alvin's office.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — The Festival and the haunted church
> During the Festival, the church is decked out with a Halloween theme and turned into a haunted house; Alvin appears dressed as a vampire by the hall door, alongside Catti, Alphys, and Vegetoid. If spoken to, he recounts how he found the vestibule wrecked after the events of Ch.4, and admits that the sincerity of the letter he received (not knowing Susie wrote it) was what finally encouraged him to start writing his own sermons, instead of just repeating his father's. He believes it was the Angel who caused the damage, describing it as "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] winds of great change."

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Gerson Boom
> His father, deceased before the start of the game. He tends his grave and preserves his legacy, though carrying the insecurity of not following in his footsteps as a writer. Gerson's final letter — rewritten by Susie — closes his arc. [[Gerson Boom]]

> [!quote]- Asriel
> As a kid, Asriel used to go to the church to "confess" made-up sins to him as a game, many of them committed in Kris's name — a memory Alvin still holds fondly even years later. It's one of the few ties Alvin directly mentions with the Dreemurr family from before the start of the game. [[Asriel]]

> [!quote]- Susie
> Although they never interact directly on screen, it's Susie who rewrites Gerson's final letter for him in Ch.4 — a gesture that indirectly connects two characters marked by the death of a father figure or mentor. Alvin never knows for certain who it was, but the gesture serves its purpose all the same. [[Susie]]

> [!quote]- Ángel
> He firmly believes in faith and the Prophecy, and treats the Angel with genuine religious reverence. In Ch.5 he even attributes the previous chapter's church damage to the Angel, describing it as "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] winds of great change." [[Ángel]]

## Theories

> [!question]- Did Alvin unknowingly open the church's Fountain?
> The Dark Fountain of the First Sanctuary in the church is notably smaller than usual, suggesting little Determination went into opening it — and the game itself establishes that whoever opens one usually does so thinking of a loved one. Part of the fandom speculates that it was Alvin himself, under some influence from the Roaring Knight, who opened it without realizing that doing so would revive his father as a Darkner. **Fan theory, unconfirmed.**
>
> ![[alvin_church_at_night_dark_fountain.png]]

## Trivia
- His name and design as a religious turtle, together with his father's legacy, work as a thematic mirror of Susie's relationship with Ralsei and Kris's with the Prophecy: children carrying stories they didn't write themselves.
- He shares a direct parallel with Tolkien and his son Christopher: unlike Christopher Tolkien, who did end up editing his father's unfinished work, Alvin never dared to continue Gerson's.
