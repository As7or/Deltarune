> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 👮 Chief of police of Hometown, in charge of keeping order and arresting the town's few "criminals."
> 🎣 A returning character from Undertale, with both eyes visible (no eyepatch) and no memory at all of Alphys, unlike her original counterpart.
> 🕳️ She got the chief of police position after Asgore was removed from it for unexplained reasons.
> 🐴 At the end of Ch.3, she tries to arrest the Roaring Knight while he attempts to kidnap Toriel — the Knight kidnaps her instead.
> 🚪 She is dragged to the Shelter, where she is trapped behind the door that closes just before [[Susie]] and Kris can get in. The lock has three fields with three symbols: a pine tree, a police badge, and the Delta Rune — by Ch.5, the first three digits of the pine tree code are known (122, found at Noelle's house), along with the full police badge code (0915199X, hidden by Asgore).
> 💪 She's strong enough to lift cars as dead weight without effort — she literally does so during a traffic jam caused by a dog spinning donuts with a toy car.
> 😆 If the Dark World is mentioned to her, she laughs so hard she breaks her own desk with a single blow, accidentally flinging the jail cell key to the door and letting the Wet-Nose Bandits she had detained escape.
> 💬 She keeps her iconic battle cry ("NGAHHHHH!!!") and her habit of calling whoever's nearby "punk."

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Just like in Undertale: energetic, loud, and eager to find real villains to face in a town where almost nothing happens — her wish comes true in the worst possible way when she runs into the Roaring Knight. Despite the tough exterior, she shows genuine concern for Alphys as soon as they get to know each other.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapters 1 and 2 — The chief of police
> She can be found directing traffic or at the police station, always ready to scold anyone who talks to her from the side or from behind. Near the library, she resolves a traffic jam by literally lifting one of the stuck cars, caused by a dog spinning donuts in a toy car — and takes the chance to announce she has some "bench-press stuff" to get to. In Ch.2 she starts showing interest in Alphys's wellbeing after learning she had a bike accident; if the Dark World is mentioned in front of her, she laughs so hard she breaks her own desk, accidentally letting the detained Wet-Nose Bandits escape. If Kris tries to warn her about the library's Dark World, Undyne tasks him with delivering a heart-shaped box of chocolates to Alphys as an apology for the accident — with a "get well soon" written in her own hand, and underneath, left unerased, the tag "from your bike" that gives away the gift is about the vehicle, not about her. If Kris doesn't remove the threatening note on the box ("[TODO: verify exact EN quote] OPEN IT AND I'LL ARREST YOU!"), Alphys remains just as confused about Undyne's real intentions.
>
> ![[Undyne_overworld_bench-press.webp]]
> *Undyne lifting one of the stuck cars next to the library — right before going off to "do bench-press."*

> [!example]+ Chapter 3 — The kidnapping
> After a call from Toriel about a flat tire, Undyne heads to the Dreemurr house just as the Roaring Knight tries to kidnap Toriel. She throws a hail of spears at him and tries to arrest him, but the Knight shifts into a winged form and flies off with her, leaving his cap behind. Susie and Kris follow a trail of spears through the Light World and reach the Shelter, but the door closes — with black smoke pouring out from inside — just before they can get in.
>
> ![[Knight_screenshot_Undyne_captured.png]]
> *The Knight capturing Undyne at the end of Ch.3, just before taking her out of the TV World and into the Shelter.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — The search
> Susie sets out to rescue her, while Kris seems to secretly resist the group gaining access to the Shelter. Before her kidnapping, Undyne invites Alphys to church, remarking that she's been in town a while and "should make more friends" — the first clear sign that their friendship, nonexistent at the start of the game, is taking root on its own.

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — Still no appearance
> She makes no appearance at the Festival: she's still trapped in the Shelter since the end of Ch.3. It's precisely during this chapter that, while Susie is looking for something to heal Flowery, Kris searches her storage container and finds some seed packets alongside another of the Shelter's codes (0915199X) — the second real step toward her rescue, still incomplete by the end of the chapter.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Roaring Knight
> Her kidnapper. It was after defeating the Delta Team that the Knight switched targets, capturing her instead of Toriel, and dragged her to the Shelter. [[Roaring Knight]]

> [!quote]- Shelter
> The place where she remains trapped from the end of Ch.3 — when the Roaring Knight kidnaps her in Toriel's place — until at least the end of Ch.4. Her rescue remains one of the plot's major pending goals, and finding the access code in Ch.4 is the first real step toward achieving it. [[Shelter]]

> [!quote]- Alphys
> Unlike in Undertale, they're practically strangers at the start of the game — but Undyne worries about her after a bike accident in Ch.2, going so far as to have Kris deliver an apology box of chocolates (though the note that comes with it makes clear it's "about the bike," not a romantic gesture), and invites her to church before Ch.4. Alphys, for her part, blushes and changes the subject as soon as the topic of the police comes up, at one point half-admitting that "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] it's no secret they're easy on the eyes" before cutting herself off. [[Alphys]]

> [!quote]- Susie
> They don't interact much on screen, but it's Susie who sets out to rescue her from the Shelter in Ch.4, becoming the personal goal that most drives the group to keep investigating the mystery of that area. [[Susie]]

> [!quote]- Toriel
> The one she tries to protect when the Roaring Knight shows up at her house at the end of Ch.3 — she ends up captured in Toriel's place when the Knight switches targets after defeating the Delta Team. She is, indirectly, the reason Undyne ends up trapped in the Shelter. [[Toriel]]

> [!quote]- Asgore
> He held the position of chief of police of Hometown before her, for reasons the game never fully explains — a small background mystery that fits the pattern of other characters (like Rudy) who knew Asgore in a life before his current, quieter one. [[Asgore]]

## Trivia
- The police tape blocking the road next to the station reads "NGAHHHHH!!!" — her battle cry from Undertale.
- Her line "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] Nothing to report, huh? At times like this... punks like you should be off playing video games" is a reference to a line from Captain Strong in EarthBound.
- If offered help because "nothing ever happens" in Hometown, she jokingly suggests someone rob the town bank so she can tackle them — then, remembering Hometown doesn't have a bank, changes the request to just building one.
