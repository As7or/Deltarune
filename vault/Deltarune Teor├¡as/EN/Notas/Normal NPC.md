> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🐦 Blue bird-monster with yellow stripes ("Striped Bird" according to some fans, "spr_normalnpc" in the game's files).
> 📚 First appears in the Ch.1 library, blocking access to the upper floor.
> 👻 Bears a notable physical resemblance to Undertale's Gaster Follower #3 — the only one of those followers with no playable "normal" (non-Goner) counterpart in that game.
> 🚧 Blocks key areas across several chapters (the library books in Ch.1 and 2, access to the Lake in Ch.4) while dropping cryptic messages.
> 🍡 At the Ch.5 Festival he runs a takoyaki (fried octopus ball) stand — a detail that has sparked a fairly dark theory about Onionsan.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Enthusiastic to the point of being suspicious: he recommends books with tremendous energy while physically blocking access to them. His speech is almost always cryptic or outright nonsensical — with the notable exception of one repeated line, verbatim "Don't wait, don't wait. Especially, the don't. Wait, don't. Wait... don't!!!", which unsettlingly matches Onionsan's speech pattern right before he disappeared.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Is he a follower of Gaster?
> Undertale's Gaster Follower #3 is the only one of the three followers with no playable "normal" (non-Goner) counterpart in that game — and Normal NPC fits almost perfectly into that visual gap. A strong theory given the nearly identical design, though the game itself never confirms it or explicitly links him to Gaster.

> [!question]- "Wait, Don't" — an acrostic for W.D. Gaster?
> A less-cited but very direct reading: the endlessly repeated line is literally "Wait, Don't" over and over — the same initials, W. D., as W. D. Gaster. Since Normal NPC is already visually tied to a Gaster Follower, the coincidence between the line's initials and Gaster's full name fits strikingly well with the character's other clues. It remains an unconfirmed reading with no official source — a coincidence of initials, not a textual citation of his name — but it's hard to ignore given the character's other existing connections to Gaster.

> [!question]- What happened to Onionsan?
> A speculative and fairly grim fan theory: the line Normal NPC repeats matches Onionsan's speech right before he disappeared, and his Ch.5 takoyaki stand "tastes like raw onion" — leading to speculation that Normal NPC may have had something to do with Onionsan's disappearance, and might even be selling his remains as food at the Festival. It's a fan theory with no direct textual support beyond that taste/dialogue coincidence — taking it as more than a dark fandom joke would go well beyond anything the game actually implies.
