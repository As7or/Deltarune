> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🥚 Blue, oval-shaped objects/"enemies" from the Cyber World, closely tied to Spamton — their own Check text calls them "clams," but Spamton insists on calling them "eggs."
> 💥 When they burst, they release dozens of tiny Spamton faces flying in every direction; they're Spamton NEO's signature attack.
> 🎀 Since v1.07 there's a 1/100 chance that "Ms. Pipis" appears in its place, identical except for a small pink bow.
> 💌 Before Ch.2, Spamton sent Noelle a digital Pipis as thanks for being the only one who read his spam mail — she installed it as a mod in the fictional game "Cat Petterz 2," and the object started multiplying on its own until the game crashed on her.
> 📺 Tenna keeps a Pipis hidden in a drawer, which he refers to with feminine pronouns and which embarrasses him — ironic, given the contempt he shows toward Spamton in public.
> 🍽️ They're referenced as food in several places: a piece of graffiti claims Tenna grinds them up to make "TV Dinners," and the Executive Buffet in Ch.3 contains "blue caviar" that's implied to be them.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> They don't seem to have any personality or will of their own outside their bond with Spamton — more a trap/weapon than a sentient creature, with no audible dialogue. The exception is the one Tenna keeps, which "chirps" and "clucks" faster and faster the closer you get to it, suggesting some emotional reactivity after all.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Spamton
> They're literally "his eggs" — he uses them as a weapon, as a healing source, and as his most iconic attack in his NEO form, where his nose turns into a beak and produces Pipis as if they were bird eggs. [[Spamton]]

> [!quote]- Noelle
> She received a digital Pipis from Spamton before Ch.2, as thanks for reading his spam mail — she put it into "Cat Petterz 2" and the object multiplied on its own until it crashed the game. This exchange is proof that Spamton and Noelle already knew each other before the player arrived. [[Noelle]]

> [!quote]- Tenna
> He keeps one hidden in a drawer, which embarrasses him despite his public contempt for Spamton — one of the most revealing details about how hypocritical their rivalry is. [[Tenna]]

> [!quote]- FRIEND
> It's speculated that the Pipis (described as "clams," a protein source) are part of what draws the FRIEND to Spamton, along with the fact that he himself is described as smelling of "meat" — which would fit the theory that Spamton actively protects himself from being hunted by them. [[FRIEND]]

> [!quote]- Forgotten Man
> Both are thematically tied to eggs and to the theory that they serve as "protein" for FRIEND — the Forgotten Man hands them out, Spamton produces them. [[Forgotten Man]]

## Theories

> [!question]+ [[Huevo|Eggs]], clams, or something else?
> Not even the game itself can agree: the Check text calls them clams, Spamton calls them eggs (and clarifies that only he has the right to call them that), and they're implied to be edible on more than one occasion. This deliberate ambiguity feeds the theory that, like much of what surrounds Spamton, not even he really knows what they actually are.

> [!question]- FRIEND's target?
> Their nature as a "protein source" (alongside Spamton himself, described as smelling of meat) puts them at the center of the theory that Spamton hides from FRIEND — the same FRIEND that has also been speculatively linked to the eggs the Forgotten Man hands out. Neither connection is directly confirmed, but they reinforce each other.

## Trivia
- There's an unused sprite of a yellow Pipis, probably scrapped because it would be confused with the yellow bullets of Kris's SOUL during the fight against Spamton NEO.
- The word "Pipis" recalls "Bepis," a typo of "Pepsi" that Toby Fox reuses as a recurring joke across several of his games.
- They're the only enemy in Ch.2 that can't be recruited.
