> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🃏 Darkner from the Card Kingdom; proclaims himself "Duke of Puzzles" and serves the King in Ch.1. According to Jigsaw Joe, when the King took power he [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "fired everyone and replaced all the puzzle-makers with just one guy... Rouxls Kaard."
> 🗣️ Speaks in an exaggerated archaic English (thick with "thou", "-eth" and "-est") — and in Ch.2 he even mixes it with a pirate accent ([TODO: verify exact EN quote] "Chips Ahoyeth, Landlubbers! Art thou-eth ready-eth to visit-eth Davith Jones' Chest-eth?"), which makes Ralsei himself ask if he could please not speak with more than one accent at a time.
> 🧩 His puzzles are ridiculously easy — pushing a box onto a button — even though he destroyed the instructions for the previous puzzles himself to seem more difficult.
> 👑 He's a recurring "miniboss for hire": in every chapter he tries to become the right hand of whoever currently rules the Dark World — the King (Ch.1), Queen (Ch.2), and Tenna (Ch.3). From Ch.4 on he's left on his own in Castle Town.
> 💜 He's the second canonically bisexual character in Deltarune, and the first to form a polyamorous relationship: in Ch.3 he tries to become Tenna's second-in-command by forming a throuple with Lanino and Elnina.
> 👶 Even though the King barely looks after Lancer, it's Rouxls who really feeds and cares for him — Lancer considers him his "lesser dad."
> ⚡ In a secret cutscene in Ch.4, he tries to sneak into the Light World chasing Queen again ([TODO: verify exact EN quote] "Princess! Where art thou? Thou canst run, but thou canst not hideth") — and ends up electrocuted, turned into a lamp ("Rouxls Lampeth").

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Self-centered, opportunistic, and completely oblivious to his own shortcomings — he believes himself fit for anything he sets his mind to, despite being incompetent at nearly everything he attempts. He betrays his superiors without hesitation whenever it benefits him, and avoids direct conflict by fleeing from the Lightners as soon as he can. Despite his treacherous front, he cares for Lancer with genuine affection that he insists on hiding. When Susie confronts him about his attempted murders in Ch.1, he replies with total seriousness that it's [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "an interesting Historyeth Theory-eth" but that [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "the Evidence-eth doth refute it-eth" — and offers her a plate of worms in exchange for her silence; Susie takes the deal without further argument.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Lancer
> Despite the King's indifference, it's Rouxls who really cares for and feeds Lancer — his "lesser dad." Lancer constantly asks him to listen to his music player, something Rouxls isn't thrilled about at all, though he never stops paying attention to him. [[Lancer]]

> [!quote]- King
> His former lord in Ch.1, whom he serves with a loyalty that's more apparent than real — Rouxls Kaard switches sides or strategy the moment it suits him, which becomes clear when in Ch.2 he tries to carve out a spot next to Queen instead of following the King. [[King]]

> [!quote]- Queen
> Tries to become her right hand in Ch.2, seeking a promotion the King never gave him — without lasting success, since Queen doesn't seem to take him too seriously either. It's a recurring pattern in his character: seeking recognition from whoever holds power at the moment. [[Queen]]

> [!quote]- Tenna
> Tries to become her second-in-command in Ch.3 by forming a throuple with Lanino and Elnina — Tenna, for her part, mocks him constantly in public. [[Tenna]]

## Theories

> [!question]- Why does he always chase after someone else's power?
> A widespread reading (a very popular fandom headcanon, with no official confirmation) is that his constant need to be the "right hand" of whoever's in charge reflects a deeper crisis of purpose as a Darkner — the search for validation from someone who never quite finds a place of his own. It's a fan interpretation, not something the game confirms directly.

## Trivia
- His name is literally pronounced "Rules Card," a pun on his true identity.
- He was going to have his own battle theme in Ch.1 ("Epic Rouxls Battle"), scrapped because Toby Fox decided Rouxls [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "is not epic at all."
- Despite wearing an eyepatch as part of his disguise, it doesn't appear in his dialogue portrait — a small graphical oversight.
