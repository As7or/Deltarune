> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🥚 A secret, optional key item, one per chapter, always handed over by the [[Forgotten Man]] in a different hidden area each time.
> 🎒 Can be "used" from the inventory at any time, but has no effect whatsoever — it's purely narrative/collectible.
> 🐱 A later Ch.5 update revealed that the Eggs are, literally, the "protein" that FRIEND chases in its cat form — the first confirmed textual link between these three elements.
> 📜 Its name and its behavior ("it sticks to you and won't let go" if you try to drop it) are a direct nod to Undertale's Entry Number 10, about the golden flowers' seeds.
> 🏆 The Ch.4 Egg intentionally causes you to lose that chapter's completion trophy/achievement on PlayStation, meant to unlock secret dialogue.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> It's not a character, but an object and a recurring narrative pattern — but, just like the [[Cristal Oscuro]], each Egg works as a small, isolated clue that only makes sense once put together with the rest: on their own they're just a curious collectible, together they point to a much more uncomfortable revelation about FRIEND and about the Forgotten Man himself.

## The Forgotten Man's "schedule" — a clue to himself
In Mancountry (Ch.3), after promising Kris he won't forget him again, the Forgotten Man shares his "schedule of availability" — a list that seems to name random, meaningless objects, but which are actually veiled clues about where to find the Eggs from the other chapters: "the donuts" (Ch.1, for the donut sale next to that area), "the dumpster" (Ch.2), "the windowsill" (Ch.4), and "harvest the wheat" (Ch.5). It's, to date, the only time the character himself gives direct, verifiable clues about his own pattern of appearances.

## The protein reveal (Ch.5)
After defeating Pink, interacting with FRIEND in Ch.5, and having collected that chapter's Egg, talking to Pink in her shop unlocks new dialogue: her possessed body takes Kris's hand and quietly explains that she once knew someone who had a whole pack of hunting cats, very good at finding "protein." She warns that if Kris has any protein on him, he has to keep it hidden, because those cats — not very smart, but incredibly persistent — never stop chasing their target until they get it, and bite very hard once they catch it. Some people, according to her, have gone as far as changing their appearance or hiding completely to escape them.

![[FRIEND.png]]
*FRIEND, whose cat form is the one that "sniffs out" the Eggs' protein according to Pink's revelation.*

This single line of dialogue suddenly connects three of the game's biggest mysteries that until then seemed only tangentially related: the Forgotten Man (who hands out the Eggs), the Eggs themselves (the "protein"), and FRIEND (the "cat" chasing it). It also confirms that FRIEND is probably more of a species than a single entity, since Pink talks about "a whole pack."

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Forgotten Man
> He's the one who hands over every Egg, always in person and always to Kris, in a different secret area each chapter — from the hidden tree in the Forest in Ch.1 onward. He's one of the few narrative patterns that connect him directly to Kris chapter after chapter, without the rest of the group ever witnessing it. [[Forgotten Man]]

> [!quote]- FRIEND
> A Ch.5 update revealed, through Pink, that the Eggs are literally the "protein" that draws FRIEND in its hunting-cat form — the first confirmed textual link between the two elements, until then only connected by fan speculation. It's one of the few times a purely optional collectible ends up having a real narrative function. [[FRIEND]]

> [!quote]- Mad Mew Mew (Pink)
> She's the one who reveals the connection between the Eggs and FRIEND, in a secret dialogue only available after meeting several specific Ch.5 conditions. [[Mad Mew Mew (Pink)]]

## Theories

> [!question]- Is the Ch.4 Egg room a child therapy room?
> A theory from an analysis video, but with a much stronger real basis than usual: in the May 2025 update to the Spamton Sweepstakes (over a month before Ch.3 and Ch.4 came out), a secret page literally titled "Therapy" was added (with the visible title "The poor children"), where you can draw with the mouse to gradually reveal the same tree as the Forgotten Man's — which links directly to the original 2022 event's Egg page. In other words: the association between the Egg, the tree, and the word "therapy" was already confirmed by Toby Fox before Ch.4's release, it's not just a visual reading of the Second Sanctuary room after the fact.
>
> The fan reading that goes a step further — that this room would represent the real therapy Kris, Asriel, and Noelle would have received as children after Dess's disappearance, and that the "tree" Kris paints over it covers a specific repressed memory (see [[FRIEND]]) — remains speculation with no direct textual confirmation within the game itself, but it starts from a much firmer basis than a simple aesthetic coincidence.

> [!question]+ Why do they "protect" Kris from FRIEND?
> If the Eggs are literally protein for FRIEND, there's room for the reading that the Forgotten Man doesn't give them to Kris as a simple collectible, but as an indirect way of protecting him — giving him something FRIEND will chase instead, or that Kris can use to divert its attention. Working against this idea is the fact that Kris never actively "drops" or uses the Egg within the game. **A fan theory, unconfirmed.**

## Trivia
- The item can't be dropped from the inventory: trying to do so shows a message saying it "sticks to everything and can't be thrown away," a textual nod that's almost a direct copy of Undertale's Entry Number 10 about the golden seeds.
- A GIF from Deltarune's Spring 2023 newsletter, showing the Annoying Dog swinging between two yellow flowers held up by a balloon, turned out to be a real, direct clue to the location of the Ch.5 Egg, months before its release.
- If you carry the Egg back to the Light World, it can be stored in specific places depending on the chapter: in Asgore's fridge in Ch.1, or in the egg basket at Sans's shop in Ch.2 ([[Susie]] remarks that Kris has "stolen from him in reverse"). Also, talking to Temmie at school lets you see her place an egg on her desk and interact with it to check how many eggs have been collected so far — the game itself keeping count of the joke out loud.
