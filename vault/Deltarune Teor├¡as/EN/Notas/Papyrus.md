*Table note: as of today, Papyrus is a completely invisible character in Deltarune. There is no sprite of him within the game itself beyond the one he already had in Undertale, reused here only for reference.*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🦴 Sans's younger brother; he lives with him in a house east of the grocery store, in Hometown — whose sprite is internally named "bg_building_paphouse".
> 👻 A character who **has never appeared in person** in any chapter to date (Ch.5 included) — one of the most discussed mysteries of the entire saga.
> 🚪 When you knock on the door of his house, the narration only reads *"(No response...) (... but the distant trousle of bones.)"* — official verbatim text; "trousle" is the game's own made-up word.
> 📅 [TODO: verify exact EN quote] Sans promises to introduce him in Ch.1; in Ch.2 he says *"i'm sure you'll meet him someday. maybe around when your brother comes home?"* and they still won't be able to meet him. He is still not mentioned by name in Ch.5's dialogue.
> 🎵 His Undertale battle theme, Bonetrousle, keeps playing (progressively more distant) at his house's door, chapter after chapter.
> 🔊 Despite having no dialogue lines, his voice sound effect is present in the files of all five chapters — his absence seems deliberate, not a simple development oversight.
> 💬 His name is one of the few that triggers a unique response from the intro narrator's voice if it's entered as Kris's name at the start of Ch.1, instead of a generic reaction.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> There is no direct basis within Deltarune to describe his personality — everything that can be said about him is inherited from Undertale: enthusiastic, naive, proud of his (questionable) culinary and detective skills, and desperate to make friends. If his absence is intentional, one could imagine that this same innocence is exactly what someone (Sans? the game itself?) would be protecting by keeping him out of sight.

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The promise
> Sans tells Kris that, since he's new in town, *"he needs friends,"* and asks to be visited at the end of the chapter to meet him. Upon reaching the door of his house, however, there is no response — only *"the distant trousle of bones."*
>
> ![[Sans's_house_screenshot_knocking.png]]
> *Kris knocking on the door of Sans and Papyrus's house in Ch.1 — no response, only the distant trousle of bones.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 2 — "I'm sure you'll meet him someday"
> Sans reports Papyrus isn't available that day, assuring Kris *"i'm sure you'll meet him someday. maybe around when your brother comes home?"* The door text this time reads *"(Faintly.... faintly, a trousle... growing further away...)"*
>
> ![[Sans's_house_screenshot_knocking_Chapter_2.png]]
> *The same door in Ch.2 — the trousle, this time, fades even further away.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Near-total silence
> The door text now reads *"(You hear nary a bone... No rattle, nor trousle, nor xylophone tickle.)"* — the faintest mention of all to date, alongside a neutral battle dialogue associated with him even though no actual battle takes place.

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — No mention
> Papyrus is not mentioned by name in any dialogue or flavor text in the chapter — the first time he isn't even named indirectly since Ch.1.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Where is he?
> The most-cited open question about Papyrus. The most common readings range from the purely comedic (Toby Fox stretching the joke out until the saga's epilogue) to darker readings that connect him to other unresolved mysteries in the game — for example, the recurring joke that "we've already met him: he's the Roaring Knight," which no fan seriously defends but which keeps getting repeated as a fandom joke.

> [!question]- Does he confuse Flowey with [[Flowery]]?
> In Undertale, Papyrus repeatedly and consistently calls Flowey "Flowery" — mixing up or misremembering his name — something Flowey himself corrects ("It's FlowEY. Without the R." — quoted verbatim in the original Spanish note). It's a real, confirmed joke, not a fan hypothesis. The coincidence that Deltarune now has a floral antagonist literally named "Flowery" has fueled the joke — this one genuinely speculative — that if Papyrus were ever to appear in Deltarune, he might confuse him with his old "friend" from Undertale for the same old reason.
>
> ![[Papyrus_flowery.png]]
> *The original joke from Undertale: Papyrus calls Flowey "Flowery," and Flowey corrects his name.*

> [!question]- Is he the [[Forgotten Man]]?
> The weakest of the three readings, held up mostly by merchandising coincidences and official trivia external to the game rather than clues within the game itself: there's an official Fangamer drawing showing Papyrus hiding behind a tree with red leaves nearly identical to the Forgotten Man's, and part of the fandom connects the cracked-egg shape that the Mystery Man's head (the sprite linked to Gaster) would evoke with Papyrus's fondness for [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "that oatmeal with dinosaur eggs," mentioned in passing by Flowey in a 2016 Undertale Q&A. Adding to this is another official Q&A image by Toby Fox, this time of Papyrus "on his date," surrounded by [TODO: verify exact EN quote] a joke stat panel (POPULATION, CRIME...) that includes an icon labeled, with no further explanation, "EGG" — another wink from the author himself linking Papyrus to an egg, even if only in the format of a joke. There is still no clue within Deltarune itself to support this reading; what exists is an accumulation of winks external to the game (merchandising, Q&As) that the fandom keeps pointing to. **A highly speculative fan theory, supported by merchandising and official trivia external to the game, not by the game itself; unconfirmed.**
>

## Trivia
- He is, along with Gaster, one of the two Undertale characters with the strongest "ghost" presence in Deltarune: constantly mentioned, never seen.
- The internal flag that checks whether Kris has talked to Sans about his younger brother is named, in the game's files, simply "paptalk".
