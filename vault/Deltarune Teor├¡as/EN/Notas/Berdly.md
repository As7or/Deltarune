> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🤓 Kris's classmate in Alphys's homeroom; the school's self-proclaimed "Number 1" and Noelle's study partner, whom he considers his only real friend.
> 🦉 Can turn his head backward like an owl — a detail frequently cited by the fandom.
> 🎮 Fan of "Super Smashing Fighters" (the in-universe equivalent of Super Smash Bros.); he even mentions wavedashing with Kris.
> 🩹 Genuinely low self-esteem beneath his arrogant front: admits he depends on Noelle as a study partner and feels he hasn't earned his reputation for being "smart" on his own.
> 🦸 In Ch.2 he goes from secondary antagonist to key ally, providing the decisive mech against Queen.
> 💘 Wrongly believes Noelle is in love with him — until Susie assures him she doesn't care whether he's smart or dumb, at which point he falls head over heels for her, nicknaming her "Gamer Girl."

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Dramatic, a know-it-all, with an ego that often clashes with reality — he's not as smart as he seems, and much of his confidence depends entirely on Noelle. As a child he was described as "forgettable," someone whose name barely anyone bothered to remember; Noelle was his only real friend from then on, and much of his need to stand out as "number 1" seems to stem from that. Despite the facade, he genuinely cares about his classmates: on the Weird Route he shows real fear for Noelle's safety, even at the cost of his own life.

## His role in the story
Berdly isn't a Darkner and has no tie to the Prophecy as a hero — his role is that of a secondary antagonist and late-arriving ally, almost a mirror of Susie herself: someone who starts on the "bad side" of Ch.2 out of insecurity and a need for recognition, and ends up decisive for the outcome. Unlike Susie, his arc isn't tied to the Dark World itself, but to his real relationship with Noelle in the Light World — he's the only character, besides Susie herself, whose fate changes radically depending on how the player treats Noelle throughout the chapter.

## Relationships

> [!quote]- Noelle
> His study partner, whom he depends on far more than he admits, and his only real friendship since childhood. He wrongly assumes she's in love with him — a misunderstanding that persists, with varying nuances, through much of the game. Despite how one-sided his treatment of her is in public, he's always been kind to her in private, and on the Weird Route he's one of the few characters who notices the power imbalance Kris holds over Noelle, and tries to stand up to protect her. [[Noelle]]

> [!quote]- Susie
> Falls head over heels for her after she assures him she doesn't care whether he's smart or dumb — he starts calling her "Gamer Girl" from then on. This initial crush is superseded by Ch.5, when he recognizes and respects Noelle's feelings for her. [[Susie]]

> [!quote]- Queen
> His ally (and, briefly, his controller) in Ch.2 — together they try to build a "Smartopia," until she loses control over him and ends up forcibly subduing him by plugging a cable into his face. Queen sees him as a nuisance almost from the start and prioritizes Noelle for her plan, something Berdly never fully notices. [[Queen]]

> [!quote]- Kris
> Classmate and occasional rival; they also share a love of fighting games, though Kris usually beats him, which infuriates him. The exact origin of their prior rivalry is unknown. [[Kris]]

## In battle
As a Ch.2 enemy, he attacks with "Chirashi" (papers with "A+" grades fired in waves) and "Spear Blast" (a burst of projectiles in a curved line). On the Weird Route, he fires more papers per wave with less time between them, making the fight noticeably more aggressive before Noelle freezes him with SnowGrave.

## Theories

> [!question]+ What's behind his paralyzed arm?
> After the Weird Route or the normal Ch.2 battle, Berdly mentions in Ch.4 that he has his own theory about why his arm is still paralyzed, which he immediately dismisses as "irrational" without explaining it — one of the few times the character himself hints at knowing more than he lets on about the Dark World's aftereffects on a Lightner's body. **Fan theory, unconfirmed.**

## Trivia
- His Dark World design is visually inspired by Falco Lombardi, from Star Fox.
- He can turn his head 180 degrees like a real owl.
- The flower Seth, in Ch.5, idolizes him as an "avian genius" without him ever finding out — a small nod to the fact that his fame, real or not, has spread further than he imagines.
- During the 2022 Spamton Sweepstakes, official content on Noelle's blog hinted that Berdly comments under a username like "smartgenius555" on the site, repeatedly asking to be made a moderator and complaining about prank calls.
