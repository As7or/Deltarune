> - ⚔️ **Narrative role:** silent protagonist, the player's de facto "vessel"; leads the party in the Dark World (equipment, inventory, orders).
> - 🍫 **Design trivia:** shares with Chara (Undertale) a fascination with knives and a taste for chocolate.

> - 🖐️ **Fan theory (unconfirmed):** several visual clues, and since Ch.5 a textual comment from Mad Mew Mew/Pink about a cat bite on their hand, fuel speculation that Kris might lose the hand later on — Pink brings it up, but never confirms it. See "Hand Theory" below.

## Personality

> [!tip]+ Personality profile
> Quiet, introverted, and low-energy — falls asleep in class and would rather have their mother drive them than walk. Speaks in a flat, mumbling voice, to the point that other characters sometimes ask them to repeat what they said — though during the events of the game, Noelle notices their voice sounds strange, [TODO: verify exact EN quote] "like it's coming from a speaker," and on the Weird Route she goes as far as saying she no longer recognizes it at all. Despite their reserve, they have a certain natural ease with flirting, though they don't usually use it with romantic intent.
>
> As the story progresses, they begin to **actively disobey** the player: changing the tone of a line, covering their mouth, coughing, or simply doing nothing. They also act on their own even with the SOUL equipped — protecting Susie from King and from the Sound of Justice, drinking juice with her at the church, or kneeling to comfort Ralsei at the 3rd Sanctuary.

### Interests
Despite their distant appearance, Kris has plenty of hobbies: they've always been mischievous (they liked hiding under Noelle's bed to scare her, and tried to flush a bath bomb down their own toilet more than once). As a kid they studied occultism with Catti, an interest they still keep — one of their internet searches is literally "how to use magic," and in their customized room in the Cyber World that search shows the result "JONGLER" (confirmed via a new Spamton Sweepstakes page from May 2025: Noelle explains in her blog that "Jongler" is the name of a cat-catching minigame from *Cat Petters* — though it's unclear why that specific search of Kris's returns exactly that result). They like video games, played a lot with Asriel as a kid, and are apparently good enough to beat Berdly at fighting games (both are mentioned "wavedashing" against each other). They have some knowledge of anime and dislike words like "tsundere."

They've had a fascination with knives since they were very young — if Noelle is given the AquaKnife, she describes it as "nostalgic" — and have searched the internet for things like "sharpest kitchen knife, jello" or ads for knives made of spaghetti. They seem to have a special attachment to one particular knife they carry with them almost always, which Toriel simply calls "YOUR knife"; it's implied they still have it in the Dark World but choose not to use it.

*Kris playing piano at the Dark Sanctuary, despite having the SOUL equipped.*

They love the piano — they look up tutorials to play video game songs, and both Toriel and the hospital nurse praise how well they play. During the events of the game they normally can't play well under the player's control, but they do manage to play versions of soundtrack themes at Noelle's house, and at the Dark Sanctuary they even play a version of "Don't Forget" on the organ despite the player's presence. They favor sweet food — Toriel's pie is their favorite — and always sneaks a chocolate out of a box of Temmie Hearts.

## Childhood and background
They were adopted as a small child by the Dreemurrs and grew up in a loving family, though as the only human child in Hometown they often felt a little out of place — they even asked their parents when their "horns would grow in," and the parents bought them a headband with red horns that they wore for months. They became very close to Asriel; the two explored the woods near town together along with their neighbors Dess and Noelle, watching TV together. They also tried to befriend Bratty (one of the "older girls"), going so far as to steal her food until she got sick.

When Toriel and Asgore divorced, Kris and Asriel stayed inseparable — Asriel used to treat them to hot chocolate at QC's Diner, something the family used to do together before. When Asriel left for college, Kris was left alone with barely any friends, though they kept talking late into the night online until Hometown's blackout.

## The parallel with Chara
Kris shares with Chara from Undertale a fascination with knives, a taste for chocolate, the same skin and hair tone, and even the same use of the pronoun "they" — both, moreover, presented as gender-neutral characters. The color palette of their Dark World cloak recalls Frisk's shirt, and their name is, per the most commonly cited readings, a triple pun: **Kris** may refer to the *kris*, an Indonesian dagger (for the knife they wield); to the root "of Christ" (their family "is or was" very religious); or be an anagram of "Frisk" without the F — and simultaneously of "risk." Their surname, Dreemurr, is confirmed via the correct answer to a TV World quiz in Ch.3.

## Theories

> [!question]- The symbolism of green: Kris's envy of Asriel?
> A symbolic-reading theory (a mix of data confirmed by the wiki + interpretation from an analysis video, not confirmed by direct dialogue). Confirmed starting point: in the room Kris shares with Asriel in Hometown, Asriel's side is full of trophies and color, while Kris's is empty with nothing of their own (see [[Asriel]]). That same contrast repeats in several other places:
> - **Green Room (TV World, Ch.3)**: the left couch, green with yellow stripes, and the right couch, blue with a crease mark, directly allude to Asriel's and Kris's beds in their real room — Asriel's with yellow stripes, Kris's folded with the same diagonal crease — as the wiki itself confirms.
> - **Kris's room in Ralsei's Castle**: unlike the real room, here the shelves are full of trophies — the same ones that belong to Asriel in Hometown — and the rug reproduces the Delta Rune with its three triangles colored according to the heroes: pink (Susie), blue (Kris), and green (Ralsei). In this "ideal" room, green is no longer Kris's absence, but the color representing their companion Ralsei.
> - If one accepts the reading (argued by an analysis video, without textual confirmation) that Ralsei visually "inherits" Asriel's green due to his strong physical resemblance, the castle's ideal room would work as an inversion of the real-life envy: here Kris does have their own trophies, and the green no longer belongs to an absent sibling, but to a present friend.
>
> Added to this is a possible evolving reading throughout the story: in Ch.1, Kris gets sentimental drinking hot chocolate that reminds them of their relationship with Asriel; in Ch.4, on throwing Asriel's old clothes to the floor at the church, Kris states that those clothes "no longer have any power" over them. An analysis video connects this moment to Ch.3's own maturity arc (when Kris is "knighted" in Card Castle's chess) as a symbolic progression toward becoming independent of their sibling's shadow — consistent with the chapter's overall tone about overcoming nostalgia, but without any textual confirmation that this is the intended reading.
>
> ![[asriel_room.png]]
> *The real room Kris shares with Asriel in Hometown: Asriel's bed (left), with trophies and photos on the shelf above, facing Kris's (right), with nothing of their own above it — the confirmed contrast this whole theory rests on.*

The parallel with the Vessel — a copy of Kris with no color or identity of its own — is one of the chapter's most-cited connections, though it isn't confirmed that it's a deliberate reference to the Vessel specifically. > [!question]- 🎲 Is Kris, to some extent, already dead?
> Deltarune never fully explains what a SOUL is within its own fiction — the only book that mentions it describes it as "the essence of our being," [TODO: verify exact EN quote] with no defined shape, very different from the closed definition Undertale gives (a heart representing the soul of a human or a monster). That deliberate ambiguity leaves an uncomfortable question open: if souls in Deltarune are something more abstract than a physical object, what exactly does it mean that the Player's red SOUL — never confirmed to be Kris's "real" soul (see the Vessel Theory above) — is the one Kris wears most of the time?
>
> Ch.5 offers the most direct clue to date: coming from Ch.4's Weird Route, Kris spends the whole night without the SOUL on, and the next morning shows up with sickly green skin, moving slowly and trembling as they walk — noticeably weak throughout the whole Festival (see the Ch.5 Weird Route above). An analysis video reads this literally: if Kris, without the Player's SOUL, starts physically deteriorating to a point close to death, that would suggest Kris can't survive on their own without someone else's soul sustaining them — whether because their own soul isn't where it should be, or because they never fully had one at all. Under that reading, the question stops being just "who controls Kris?" and becomes "what exactly is a human without their own soul?" — a considerably darker answer than the simple possession metaphor. **Fan theory, supported by a real, widely cited Ch.5 scene, but dependent on assuming how far Undertale's rules about SOULs apply as-is to Deltarune — something the game itself leaves deliberately ambiguous.**
>

> [!question]- Is Kris the Knight themself?
> At the end of Chapter 2, Kris opens a Dark Fountain on their own using their knife and, apparently, their own Determination — the same kind of will Queen suggests is needed for it. The game doesn't clarify whether this makes Kris a kind of Knight in their own right, in parallel with (or in conflict with) the theory that the Knight is Dess.
>
> 🎲 **The "fused Knight" theory (Amalgam Knight)**, popularized in 2026 by a J.R. Fentady video (see [[Roaring Knight]]): proposes that the Knight isn't Kris alone, but the result of a fusion with [[Noelle]] — the only other person the game itself has ever linked to the title of Knight, through Queen — occurring during the scene where the two sink hand-in-hand into the lake at the end of Ch.5's Weird Route. Under that reading, this Ch.2 moment doesn't contradict or compete with the Dess theory on its own — it opens a third possibility: that more than one person may have "been" the Knight at different points in the story.
> - For: the Knight shares poses and attacks with Kris, while their armor's silhouette resembles Noelle's "Shriek" form; they also wear reindeer horns, and their examine dialogue says Kris "couldn't learn anything" about them, leaving the door open to Kris already knowing them somehow. The most-cited clue, though, comes from the Shadow Mantle minigame itself in Ch.3: the enemy that copies the player's movements on screen is, in reality, a black deer sprite — read as a visual representation of the Knight and Kris being, deep down, the same entity.

There's also a claim circulating that the Knight's scream sounds like the Snowgrave sound effect, but that was already debunked in earlier analyses: the scream is actually a sped-up spoken voice, deep in tone, distinct from Kris's or Noelle's. **A very recent and elaborate fan theory, with no direct textual confirmation — it coexists with the Dess Knight theory without fully ruling it out.**

Putting both things together, part of the fandom reads this vision as a broken dream of Kris's own related to Noelle, not just a neutral glimpse of the Light World. **Speculative fan theory: the vision and Seam's explanation are each confirmed separately, but the game never directly states that this particular crystal is Kris's "broken dream" or that it has to do with romantic feelings toward Noelle.**

## In battle
Leads the party in the Dark World (equipment, inventory, orders). As a human, they can't cast spells — instead they use ACTs, including some exclusive ones: **Encourage** (raises the party's defense, only unlockable against King), **Pirouette** (against Jevil, with a different effect each turn), **HealDeal** (accepts a deal from Spamton to heal), **Take House 2** (against Rouxls Kaard), **X-Slash/FriedPipis** (against Spamton NEO, Weird Route only), and several others specific to each boss. Their special attack X-Slash hits twice in the same turn and deals more damage than Fight.

## Creation and development
One of the earliest sketches of Kris is from 2014 or 2015, shared by Toby Fox on his Twitter in 2018. Gigi helped with their design and their various outfits; Chess worked on the Dark World color palette and created base sprites for Temmie Chang to develop further, including their battle idle animation. During Undertale's 6th anniversary stream, Toby Fox mentioned that the scene of Susie threatening Kris at the start of the game was inspired by a real-life bully he knew who knew his mother. In a 2023 interview about *Live A Live*, Toby implied that Kris might be inspired by Oersted, a character with a more complex role than a simple "hero."
