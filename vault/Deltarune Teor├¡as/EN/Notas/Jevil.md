> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🃏 Former court jester of the Card Kingdom, secret boss and first known holder of a Dark Crystal, in Ch.1.
> 🔒 Locked up by his own friend Seam, the court magician, after he went mad and started saying things that "didn't fully make sense, but weren't complete nonsense either." [TODO: verify exact EN quote]
> 🎡 Believes the whole world is just a game with fixed rules — and that precisely because of that, locked up and all, he's the only one truly free; according to him, everyone else is "trapped" without knowing it.
> 👑 Before his madness, he was the one who told King about the glory of the Roaring Knight and the Dark Fountain — the spark that lit King's ambition and downfall (revealed in Ch.4).
> 💬 Always speaks in all caps, repeats words twice ("CHAOS, CHAOS," "FREE, FREE"), and has a strange cadence that sometimes rhymes, a leftover from his old job as a jester.
> 🃏 His real form in the Light World is the Joker card — hence why he uses attacks from all four suits without being limited to any one, like a wild card among playing cards.
> 🔷 First known holder of a Dark Crystal, "awakened" by Somebody Strange — the same pattern later repeated with Spamton, the Roaring Knight, Gerson Boom, and Pink.

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> Before his madness, it's implied he had a fun-loving character and enjoyed playing with Seam, his only real companion. After meeting Somebody Strange he keeps his vitality and eccentricity, but drifts into a playful sadism: he experiences the fight against the group as a simple "game of numbers" and genuinely enjoys the chaos he causes. Despite his cynicism — he considers everyone else "trapped" without knowing it — a real loneliness shows through beneath the facade: he admits he feels lonely in his "little freedom" and wishes more people were "free" alongside him.

## Who he is
A Darkner from the Card Kingdom, King's former court jester. After an encounter with a mysterious figure, his whole way of seeing the world changed, and he ended up locked in a hidden apartment beneath Card Castle by his own friend and playmate, Seam. He's Chapter 1's secret boss and the first known holder of a Dark Crystal.

## Theories

> [!question]+ Is "Somebody Strange" Gaster?
> The most widespread theory about who drove Jevil (and later Spamton) mad points to W. D. Gaster, the real missing scientist from Undertale. The main clue: using Kris's phone in the Dark World plays the same ambience as Undertale's Entry Number 17 — associated with Gaster's attempts at communication — described the same way the Addisons describe the call that ruined Spamton: "nothing but garbage noise." [TODO: verify exact EN quote] A strong theory given the accumulation of sound and text clues, but never directly confirmed by the game. (See also the equivalent theory in [[Cristal Oscuro]].)

> [!question]- The "increasingly Lightner" pattern
> A strong theory, already documented in the Dark Crystal note: Dark Crystal holders become progressively less "pure Darkner" with each chapter. Jevil, the first, is a completely ordinary Darkner from head to toe — unlike Spamton (halfway there), the Roaring Knight (half Lightner), or Gerson Boom (a deceased Lightner). It remains unconfirmed where this pattern is heading toward Ch.6, but Jevil functions as the purest "starting point" of the scale. [[Cristal Oscuro]]

> [!question]- Is Jevil the origin of Susie's "Weird Route"?
> The fandom connects several mechanical details — Ch.3's Bet30 ("the JOKER's bet"), Ch.5's Scythemare (accompanied by his laugh), and the fact that having the Devilsknife equipped during the Ch.4 sermon makes Susie call it "a weird card" [TODO: verify exact EN quote] — as signs that Jevil's influence lives on in Susie's combat kit long after his defeat, almost as if part of his chaos "rubbed off" on her. A fan theory, supported by several mechanical coincidences; not narratively confirmed.

> [!question]- The design: why he's the Joker
> His real identity in the Light World — the Joker card, according to the Dark World Objects list itself — mechanically explains why he isn't limited to a single suit in combat, just as the Joker can stand in for any card in many games. His confinement, moreover, reads as a parallel to the Joker's own exclusion from the deck in most traditional card games — part of the original design by kanotynes, who also designed Lancer, Rudinn, Hathy, Clover, and King.

## Trivia
- He's one of the few Deltarune characters with real voice acting, provided by Toby Fox — like Spamton, Queen, Tenna, Flowery, and Tasque Manager.
- His voice clips ("CHAOS, CHAOS!", "I CAN DO ANYTHING!", "METAMORPHOSIS!") are inspired by the character "Joker" from the fighting game *Fighters Destiny*.
- The carousel in the background of his fight is a real photograph of a carousel from "The Hoppings," a traveling fair in Newcastle upon Tyne.
- In the Ch.1 demo for Nintendo Switch, his scythe attacks killed in one hit — a bug that, according to Toby Fox, went unnoticed at the time.
- Before version 1.07 of the game, defeating Jevil didn't give the Dark Crystal right away.
- His name is probably a visual acronym of "joker," "jester," "devil," and/or "evil."
