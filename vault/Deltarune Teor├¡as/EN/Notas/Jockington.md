*Table note: Jockington doesn't have a confirmed Darkner form of his own, though he does have a reserved room in Queen's Mansion ("...it's beautiful," according to the narration).*

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🐍 A limbless snake monster with a cap and light blue shirt, dark sunglasses, and the classic "friendly jock" look of the school.
> 🏃 Member of the cross country team alongside Noelle, and classmate of Kris and Susie in Alphys's classroom.
> 💬 His way of speaking adds unnecessary commas and capitalizes random words — his own particular stylistic trademark.
> 💛 Best friend (and boyfriend) of Catti Cattenheimer since they met on hula-hoop day in gym class.
> 🏠 He has his own room in Queen's Mansion, described simply as "beautiful" upon inspecting it.
> 🤝 Unlike Catti, he openly supports the friendship between Kris and Susie — he even compares their bond to what he himself has with Catti.
> 🔮 He is the only character named directly and literally in the Prophecy ("JOCKINGTON GROWS THE BEARD"), and the first Lightner to fulfill his own part of the prophetic text — in a completely comedic way.
>
> ![[Jockington_overworld.webp]]
> *Jockington in the Light World, with his characteristic cap and sunglasses.*

> [!tip]- Personality profile
> The classic "friendly jock" of the school: friendly with everyone, though it's hinted that he was initially somewhat afraid of Susie. He has an unexpectedly wise side when spoken to at the QC diner, dishing out metaphorical life advice ("[TODO: verify exact EN quote] Life's a big game of baseball, and when the batter's out on the field, he hits a big smash. Try to be that smash."). Despite his laid-back tone, he makes it clear he won't tolerate Catti's feelings being hurt under any circumstances. Catti herself describes him as curious and golden-hearted, "but not very bright" — and Toby Fox has said live that Jockington is, within the Deltarune universe itself, the most popular character in all of Hometown.
>

## Chapter-by-chapter history

> [!example]+ Chapter 1 — The classroom jock
> Appears as a secondary classmate, with his "cool" personality already established, and implicitly somewhat intimidated by Susie.

> [!example]+ Chapter 4 — Catti's tree and the warning to Kris
> Accompanies Catti when her whole family gets trapped in a tree. He congratulates Kris and Susie on their new friendship, comparing it to his own with Catti — but after learning they're going to Noelle's house, he warns Kris that he won't tolerate Catti's feelings being hurt because of it, subtly implying he'd retaliate. If spoken to a second time, he instead jokes that he'll neutralize the spell Catti would have cast on them, and recommends they "run while [they] still have legs."
>

> *Jockington saying goodbye to Kris next to Catti's tree.*
>
> > [!example]- "JOCKINGTON GROWS THE BEARD" — the Prophecy fragment
> > At the start of the Dark Sanctuary there is a glass panel of the Prophecy that literally reads "JOCKINGTON GROWS THE BEARD." A Wafer standing guard next to the panel quotes his ancestors: "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] When five o'clock arrives, the shadow shall grow" — a play on the English "five o'clock shadow," the beard shadow that appears late in the afternoon. Although it's presented as a comedic gag, a much more hidden second version of the same fragment exists in the 3rd Sanctuary (found on the middle tile of the third stretch of PrincessRBN's climbing wall), recited in a much more solemn tone by an Organikk philosopher: *"[TODO: verify exact EN quote] Before the clock strikes midnight, the shadow shall grow! Oh, the shadow shall grow, serpentine. Even as the clock turned back, the shadow kept growing. A patch of shadow, sliding, sliding, serpentine."*
> >
> > ![[Jockington_glass_beard.png]]
> > *The Prophecy's glass panel that literally announces that Jockington's beard will grow.*

> [!example]+ Chapter 5 — Festival Day: the Prophecy comes true, literally
> He attends the Festival and can be found near the King and Queen vote, where he can see Kris together with Susie and Noelle. Upon seeing Noelle, Jockington calls out to her and tells her she's about to win the title of Festival King and Queen at the same time — somewhat confusingly attributing those votes to herself, whom he thanks "a million" times in a row.
>
> Puffed up with pride over this (supposed) double triumph, he proclaims that the moment is the perfect trigger for facial hair to grow — and, right after, a beard instantly grows on him. He almost immediately regrets it and asks to have it shaved off, lamenting that nothing can be done now because the beard "has already grown." It is, by far, the most literal and least dramatic fulfillment of any Prophecy fragment in the whole game — and the one that makes Jockington the first Lightner to fulfill his part of the prophetic text directly, years after Toby Fox had already hinted at it during Undertale's 6th anniversary broadcast (2021): "[TODO: verify exact EN quote] No spoilers. Jockington grows the beard. Just kidding, sorry."
>

## Trivia
- He's one of the few Lightners with his own already-decorated room inside Queen's Mansion, with no narrative explanation as to why.
- His fragment on the Prophecy's tapestries is one of the few moments of humor within an otherwise fairly solemn text.
- In the Spamton Sweepstakes (2022), he appears as an administrator user of Noelle's blog comments under the name "sports," with a soccer ball as his avatar. Upon discovering that there can't be a soccer ball in Dragon Blazers, he announces he'll stop using the website.
- In the 2024 winter newsletter (Valentine's Day edition), he was the recipient of one of the extremely rare postcards — and true to his direct style, he only wrote "Tennis Ball" as the message.
- A baseball cap "definitely worn backwards" in the dog shrine of the Xbox version of Undertale, internally nicknamed "SerpentHelm," appears to be a reference to his cap — a retroactive nod, since Undertale predates Jockington's existence as a character by a long time.
- Before the official announcement of Chapters 3 and 4, a black-and-white version of his sprite leaked with a dialogue bubble confirming the game was in bug-testing phase.

## Theories

> [!question]- Why does his part of the Prophecy come true so literally?
> It's striking that, of the entire Prophecy, the only part that comes true clearly, directly, and verifiably as of today is precisely the most absurd and least significant one — Jockington's beard — while the rest of the characters (Gerson Boom included, with his "you have to read between the lines") insist that the prophetic text admits many readings and shouldn't be taken literally. One fandom reading sees this as a deliberate joke by Toby Fox: proving that the Prophecy really can come true exactly as written, but deliberately choosing the fragment with the least possible narrative weight to do so, leaving it open what will happen the day an important part comes true literally. See [[Profecía|Prophecy]] and [[Gerson Boom]].
