# Corkboard Connections

> [!info]+ Quick facts
> 🕸️ This note exists so the main canvas can have short, readable labels without losing the detail of why each connection is classified the way it is.
> 🎨 The color in parentheses is the same reliability code used in the canvas: 🟢 official/confirmed, 🟡 strong theory, 🔴 weak/speculative theory.
> 🔎 It's organized by thematic blocks, not alphabetically — this way it reads more like a walk through the corkboard.

## Dreemurr / Holiday Family

- **Toriel ↔ Kris** (🟢 mother): Kris's mother; ruler of Hometown alongside Asgore before their divorce.
- **Toriel ↔ Asriel** (🟢 mother): Asriel's biological mother, whom she believed lost before his return in Ch.4.
- **Asgore ↔ Kris** (🟢 father): Kris's father; owner of the flower shop that becomes the Ch.5 Dark World.
- **Asgore ↔ Asriel** (🟢 father): Their reunion is one of the central emotional moments of Ch.4.
- **Asgore ↔ Toriel** (🟢 Divorced): Ex-husband and ex-wife; they divorced before the game begins, though they still coordinate raising Kris and Asriel.
- **Asriel ↔ Kris** (🟢 Siblings): Asriel is Kris's older sibling, biological child of Asgore and Toriel; his return in Ch.4 reshapes much of the Dreemurr family.
- **Rudy ↔ Dess** (🟢 father): Dess and Noelle's father; hospitalized since Ch.5, which has fueled theories about his possible relation to the Dark World phenomenon.
- **Rudy ↔ Noelle** (🟢 father): The same Rudy, father of Noelle and Dess; his hospitalization keeps him distant from the rest of the family plot.
- **Rudy ↔ Carol** (🟢 married): Dess and Noelle's parents, a marriage established before the game begins.
- **Carol ↔ Dess** (🟢 mother): Dess's mother; the town's mayor, with a relationship marked by the pressure to keep up appearances after her disappearance.
- **Carol ↔ Noelle** (🟢 mother): Noelle's mother and the town's mayor; her relationship with Noelle is marked by that same pressure to keep up appearances.
- **Dess ↔ Noelle** (🟢 sisters): Dess is Noelle's older sister, who disappeared before the game begins; her absence shapes much of Noelle's emotional arc.

## Friendships and romances

- **Kris ↔ Susie** (🟢 partners since Ch.1): The original duo before Ralsei joins them.
- **Kris ↔ Noelle** (🟢 Friends): Childhood friends and classmates; Noelle is one of the few Lightners who shows genuine concern about Kris's increasingly strange behavior.
- **Noelle ↔ Kris** (🟢 confronts him, Ch.5): In Ch.5, Noelle confronts Kris by the Lake about his behavior and confesses that she too changed because of him.
- **Noelle ↔ Susie** (🟢 couple, confirmed Ch.5): They start with a subtle mutual interest in earlier chapters; Ch.5 confirms it unambiguously: Susie gets ready for a date with Noelle at the Festival and they spend the day together up to the Lake.
- **Ralsei ↔ Kris** (🟢 companion, tie to the Vessel): Ralsei is Kris's constant companion since Ch.1 and the one who best explains out loud the rules of the Dark World and of the vessel/SOUL.
- **Asriel ↔ Dess** (🟡 Lovers?): Unconfirmed theory that they might have had a relationship before Dess's disappearance, supported by loose environmental clues rather than direct dialogue.

## Cyber World / TV World — Spamton, Tenna, Mike, FRIEND

- **Tenna ↔ Spamton** (🟢 former business partners, now rivals): Former business partners, now archrivals — Tenna admits in Ch.5 that she knew the contract shouldn't be signed and pressured him into it anyway.
- **Tenna ↔ Kris** (🔴 secret pact): Accidentally revealed on the Ch.3 Sword Route: Tenna acts according to an agreement with Kris, without the player having ordered it directly.
- **Tenna ↔ Toriel** (🔴 kept her locked up): She secretly kept Toriel locked in a gachapon capsule during Ch.3, calling her her "ace up the sleeve" for being the one who appreciated her most.
- **Tenna ↔ Mike** (🟢 boss): Mike is Tenna's employee at TV Time; in Ch.5, Tenna mistakes him for Battat while confessing her guilt about Spamton to him.
- **Mike ↔ Spamton** (🟢 friends): Mike tried to warn him not to sign the contract and left the TV World to try to find him after his escape.
- **Mike ↔ FRIEND** (🔴 minor mysterious NPC): Mike reacts oddly to mentions of FRIEND, fueling the theory that he knows more than he lets on about that character.
- **Mike ↔ Everyman** (🔴 "easter egg" category): Both belong to the official wiki's "Easter Egg" character category: minor cameos inherited from Undertale or from the merchandise itself.
- **Spamton ↔ FRIEND** (🟡 hiding from FRIEND?): Spamton's glasses and anti-Cat armor share colors with FRIEND; Pink confirms in Ch.5 that people camouflage themselves to avoid the FRIEND cats.
- **Spamton ↔ Everyman** (🔴 merchandise-linked cameos): Both are minor nods tied to the series' real merchandising rather than to the main plot.
- **Spamton ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟡 Freedom): Spamton predicts on the Weird Route that he'll give Kris "freedom" in exchange for a broken home — the same keyword used by other Dark Crystal bearers.

## Dark Crystal-bearing bosses

- **Jevil ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟡 Dark Crystal pattern): Theoretical pattern: the Dark Crystal-bearing bosses become progressively more "Lightner" — Jevil is a normal Darkner, Spamton is halfway there, and the Roaring Knight seems half Lightner, half Darkner.
- **Jevil ↔ Spamton** (🟡 both carry a Dark Crystal): Both are secret bosses awakened by Strange Someone through a Dark Crystal, and both end up seeking to "free themselves" from the script's fixed rules.
- **Jevil ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟢 first known bearer): He's the first of the five confirmed bearers, one per chapter.
- **Roaring Knight ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟢 third bearer): The only one of the five whose battle and appearance aren't hidden — it's mandatory to progress through Ch.3.
- **Gerson Boom ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟢 fourth bearer): As Hammer of Justice, his delivery is the first time Seam describes feeling "hope" instead of just worry.
- **Jevil ↔ Noelle** (🔴 does the Devilsknife recognize her?): Unconfirmed forum theory: if the Devilsknife (Jevil's weapon) is given to Noelle, she comments that "it's smiling at her" — some read this as a nod that the weapon retains part of his consciousness.
- **Dark Crystal ↔ Player** (🟡 freeing themselves from the script): Dark Crystal bearers seek to "free themselves" from the game's pre-set script, a desire that directly mirrors the Player's conflict over control of the story.

## Gerson, Flowery — new to the corkboard

- **Gerson Boom ↔ Susie** (🟢 mentor, Ch.4): Susie's mentor in Ch.4 as the Old Man/Hammer of Justice; his battle evokes his role as Undyne's inspiration in Undertale.
- **Gerson Boom ↔ Prophecy** (🟢 wrote the Lord of the Hammer saga): He wrote the saga based directly on the Prophecy; he encourages Susie and Kris to "read between the lines" instead of following it to the letter.
- **Gerson Boom ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 revived by the Knight): He was revived as a Darkner thanks to the Dark Sanctuaries created by the Roaring Knight himself in Ch.4 — his existence directly depends on the Knight's arrival.
- **Flowery ↔ Asgore** (🟢 wants to make him happy): Formerly Asgore and Toriel's wedding bouquet; gains sentience in the flower shop's Dark World and spends all of Ch.5 trying to make him happy, even though he rejects her affection.
- **Flowery ↔ Ralsei** (🟢 archrival): Ralsei attacks with fire magic for the first time in the game, in a jealous outburst toward Flowery that the community has turned into a meme.
- **Flowery ↔ Kris** (🟢 Ch.5 final boss): Unlike previous chapters, she's the only final boss who must be defeated non-violently.

## Undyne, Alphys, and Mad Mew Mew (Pink)

- **Undyne ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 kidnapped at the end of Ch.3): After defeating the Delta Team, the Knight changes targets and captures her instead of Toriel.
- **Undyne ↔ Shelter** (🟢 trapped since Ch.3): She's dragged here, and the door closes before Susie and Kris can get in.
- **Undyne ↔ Toriel** (🟢 tries to protect her): She tries to arrest the Knight as he goes after Toriel, and ends up kidnapped in her place.
- **Undyne ↔ Alphys** (🟢 friendship built from scratch): Unlike Undertale, they barely know each other at the start of the game — Undyne worries about her after a bike accident and invites her to the church before Ch.4.
- **Undyne ↔ Asgore** (🟡 held the position before her): He was chief of police before Undyne, for reasons the game never explains.
- **Alphys ↔ Kris** (🟢 his teacher): She worries about his behavior and mentions it to Toriel.
- **Alphys ↔ Asgore** (🟢 friends): He gives her flowers in exchange for news about Kris; they share a fondness for Mew Mew Kissy Cutie.
- **Alphys ↔ Mad Mew Mew (Pink)** (🟢 gave him the figure): The gift she gave Asgore is, without her knowing, the origin of everything that happens to Pink in Ch.5.
- **Mad Mew Mew (Pink) ↔ Asgore** (🟢 his shop mascot): He keeps the figure as the Flower King's mascot, not knowing what it hides until the Dark World brings it to life.
- **Mad Mew Mew (Pink) ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟢 fifth bearer): The most hidden of the five known bearers, behind the pink door at Top of Castle.
- **Mad Mew Mew (Pink) ↔ Kris** (🟡 control parallel): Both are a shared body with an external entity that can end up separating from it.

## Queen, Seam, Ramb, Napstablook, Catti, and Jockington

- **Queen ↔ Berdly** (🟢 ally and victim, Ch.2): She wires a cable into his face to control him after losing patience with him.
- **Queen ↔ Noelle** (🟢 kidnaps her): She captures her as soon as she arrives in the Cyber World, trying to turn her into her "volunteer pawn."
- **Queen ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 her role model): She becomes obsessed with creating her own Dark Fountain after seeing him create his, until Ralsei explains to her what the Roaring entails.
- **Seam ↔ Jevil** (🟢 best friend and counterpart): He's the one who had to seal him away, and still asks about him chapters later.
- **Seam ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟢 receives them all): The only character who studies every Dark Crystal as it's obtained.
- **Ramb ↔ Kris** (🟢 treated him like a toy): As a child, Kris (and Asriel) used to take him out of the library to play with him, which made him feel special.
- **Ramb ↔ Tenna** (🟢 "two stuck in the past"): They remember playing together as kids with Kris and Asriel; a Pippin compares them directly.
- **Napstablook ↔ Undyne** (🟢 police coworkers): They work together at the Hometown police station.
- **Napstablook ↔ Mad Mew Mew (Pink)** (🟢 cousins): They live together at the Blook House along with Nobody.
- **Catti ↔ Jockington** (🟢 couple): They met on hula-hoop day in gym class.
- **Catti ↔ Kris** (🟢 blames him for hanging out with Susie): She considers Susie "the enemy."
- **Jockington ↔ Susie** (🟢 supports her friendship with Kris): Unlike Catti, he doesn't see anything wrong with their closeness.

## Rouxls Kaard

- **Rouxls Kaard ↔ Lancer** (🟢 'lesser father'): He's the one who actually cares for and feeds him, despite the King's constant indifference.
- **Rouxls Kaard ↔ King** (🟢 his lord in Ch.1): He serves him with a loyalty as self-interested as it is unreliable.
- **Rouxls Kaard ↔ Queen** (🟢 tries to be her right hand): Without lasting success, in Ch.2.
- **Rouxls Kaard ↔ Tenna** (🟢 tries to be her second-in-command): He forms a trio with Lanino and Elnina in Ch.3 to try it; Tenna mocks him publicly.

- **Asriel ↔ Player** (🟡 the same textual block): While snooping in Asriel's room, the narration says "you saw nothing"; when checking the Roaring Knight in battle, it says "Kris couldn't learn anything" — same structure, same subject (Kris), twice in the entire game. This doesn't confirm both instances are connected to each other, but it's one of the game's most solid textual coincidences.
- **Kris ↔ Spamton** (🟢 they meet in the alley): Kris is visibly affected after the battle against Spamton NEO, upon hearing him talk about being a puppet and about his fate.
- **Tenna ↔ Ralsei and Susie** (🟢 favorite contestants): She loves having them on her show; Susie later promises to find him a new owner, a promise she fulfills together with Kris.
- **Tenna ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 the broken deal): She makes a deal with him to hold onto Lightners in exchange for regaining ratings; when she breaks it by freeing Toriel, the Knight cuts off her arms as punishment.

## Pipis (Spamton's eggs — not to be confused with the Pippins)

- **Pipis ↔ Spamton** (🟢 they're literally his eggs): His most iconic attack as Spamton NEO; they also serve to heal.
- **Pipis ↔ Noelle** (🟢 they already knew each other before Ch.2): Spamton sent her a digital Pipis as thanks for reading his spam mail, long before the player reaches the Cyber World.
- **Pipis ↔ Tenna** (🟢 secretly keeps one): Despite her public disdain for Spamton, she keeps a Pipis in a drawer, one she's embarrassed about.
- **Pipis ↔ FRIEND** (🟡 possible "protein" source): They're described as a kind of clam/food, which fits the theory that Spamton is hiding from FRIEND.
- **Pipis ↔ Forgotten Man** (🟡 same egg motif): Both characters/objects share the egg motif speculatively linked to FRIEND.

## Lancer, King, Pippins, and ERAM

- **Lancer ↔ Susie** (🟢 best friend): She carries him piggyback and protects him; he admires her deeply.
- **Lancer ↔ King** (🟢 father and son): A distant relationship — King rarely spends time with him or worries about his well-being.
- **Lancer ↔ Queen** (🟡 mother, unclear): Queen herself says the relationship was "decided arbitrarily" the day the topic came up.
- **King ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 serves his new purpose): After the Knight's arrival, King proclaims himself absolute ruler and says he serves him directly.
- **King ↔ Jevil** (🟢 the spark of his villainy): Before his imprisonment, Jevil told King about the Knight's glory.
- **Pippins ↔ Mike** (🟢 Battat is a Pippin): Small Mike/Battat is actually a green Pippin in disguise, the only one of that color in the whole game.
- **Pippins ↔ Tenna** (🟢 enemy of her kingdom): They're the common enemy of the TV World, the kingdom Tenna rules.
- **ERAM ↔ Kris** (🟢 unsettling familiarity): It speaks to him with deep knowledge of the Prophecy and the game's hidden paths.
- **ERAM ↔ Ramb** (🟢 same final stretch): Both are part of the Original Game unlocked in the Green Room.
- **ERAM ↔ FRIEND** (🟡 distinct entities): FRIEND appears as a support enemy in the battle against ERAM, but they aren't the same character — an easy mistake to make, already corrected in FRIEND's note.
- **ERAM ↔ Dark Crystal** (🟡 same thematic circle): It carries the Shadow Mantle, an object distinct from the Dark Crystal but linked to the same "Strange Someone" circle.

## Sans and Papyrus

- **Sans ↔ Toriel** (🟢 became friends the night before Ch.1): Sans mentions having "become friends" with Toriel the night before the game begins; Ch.5 confirms it by showing them dancing together at the end of the Festival.
- **Sans ↔ Kris** (🟢 gets the ice cream key to the Lake): He gives Kris a fake phone number as a joke, and in Ch.5 he's the one who gets the ice cream needed for Susie and Noelle to agree to go to the Lake.
- **Sans ↔ Undertale Connection** (🟡 does the Weird/Sword Route connect both games?): A widespread theory (even picked up by the press) that completing the Weird/Sword Route to the end — including the Twisted Sword — might be what's needed to meet Gaster; a more extreme variant suggests Deltarune exists because Sans, in the future, travels back in time to prevent the human-monster war.
- **Sans ↔ Lake** (🟢 the ferris wheel that never reaches the lake): Confirmed by the game itself — the ferris wheel Sans operates at the Festival "only goes up halfway," and whoever rides it comments afterward that they wish they could have seen the Lake from up there. It's never explained why it stops there; whether it's deliberate or a simple malfunction remains speculative territory.
- **Sans ↔ Undertale Connection** (🟢 character returning from Undertale): His design, voice, and sense of humor carry over almost intact from the original game.
- **Sans ↔ Gaster** (🟡 the drawing and the inherited workshop): There's no confirmed kinship, but there is a much-cited object connection: Sans's hidden workshop in Undertale (inheriting Gaster's research into timelines) hides a photo of "three people smiling" with the phrase "don't forget" — the same motif that reappears almost identically in the card Susie draws in Deltarune's Ch.5. A more widespread reading even suggests Sans might have been a kind of apprentice to Gaster before his disappearance.
- **Sans ↔ Papyrus** (🟢 brothers): Papyrus lives with him, but Sans systematically avoids letting Kris meet him in person, chapter after chapter.
- **Papyrus ↔ Gaster** (🔴 the same absence): Both share the nature of a "mystery character" never seen on screen — without this implying any confirmed relationship between them.
- **Papyrus ↔ Roaring Knight** (🔴 fandom joke): "We already met him, it's the Knight" is a recurring fandom joke, with no serious basis.

## Berdly

- **Berdly ↔ Noelle** (🟢 study partners): He depends on her as a study partner and mistakenly believes she's in love with him.
- **Berdly ↔ Susie** (🟢 falls for her): He falls head over heels after she tells him she doesn't care whether he's smart or dumb, nicknaming her "Gamer Girl" from then on.
- **Berdly ↔ Kris** (🟢 classmates): Occasional rivals, they also share a fondness for fighting video games.

## Undertale and the Voice

- **Gaster ↔ Undertale Connection** (🟡 original UT character): Gaster is an original Undertale character who has never appeared in person in Deltarune; his presence here is purely inferred from clues.
- **Gaster ↔ Kris** (🟡 creates the Vessel?): Strong theory: the Voice (Gaster?) would be the one who creates the Vessel in the intro sequence, tying it directly to Kris's origin.
- **Gaster ↔ Shelter** (🟡 tied to the incident): The Shelter appears very close to where the Dess incident is believed to have occurred, and shares iconography with the clues associated with Gaster (static, references to "Entry 17").
- **Normal NPC ↔ Gaster** (🟡 follower?): Its design closely resembles Undertale's Gaster Follower #3, which has led to theorizing it could be a nod to those followers.
- **Undertale Connection ↔ Asriel** (🟢 shared character): Asriel is the same character as in Undertale, confirming that both games share a universe and cast rather than being separate reboots.
- **Undertale Connection ↔ Kris** (🟢 SOULs and Vessel theory): The existence of SOULs in both games fuels the theory that Kris (the vessel) was created with a mechanism similar to Undertale's.
- **Asgore ↔ Undertale Connection** (🟢 no label): Asgore is the same character (and the same name) as in Undertale, ruling both worlds as king of the monsters.
- **Toriel ↔ Undertale Connection** (🟢 no label): Toriel is also a character shared with Undertale, reinforcing that both games take place in the same universe.

## Prophecy, Routes, and the Player/Angel

- **Angel ↔ Prophecy** (🟢 prophesied figure): The Prophecy directly describes the Angel as the figure whose "Heaven" must be banished.
- **Player ↔ Kris** (🟡 who controls whom?): Theory about who really controls Kris's body: the game itself acknowledges the tension between the Player and Kris, especially at the end of Ch.2.
- **Noelle ↔ Angel** (🟡 the ARG clock): A real merchandising clue (Spamton Sweepstakes 2022) that marks her as a candidate to be the Prophecy's Angel herself.
- **Noelle ↔ Player** (🟢 also perceives him): Noelle is one of the few Lightners who seems to consciously perceive the Player, mentioning him in optional dialogue.
- **Prophecy ↔ Routes** (🟢 each route reinterprets the sacrifice): The Prophecy announces a final sacrifice to seal the Dark World; each route (Normal, Weird, Sword) reinterprets what that sacrifice means.
- **FRIEND ↔ Prophecy** (🟡 Hell's Tail): FRIEND has been linked to "Hell's Tail," mentioned in cryptic texts related to the Prophecy, though the exact meaning remains unconfirmed.

## Lake, Shelter, Dess, and the Roaring Knight

- **Dess ↔ Lake** (🔴 possible whereabouts, Ch.5): Theory that Dess might still be alive near the Lake, the central setting of Ch.5 and the area where the Roaring Knight already emerged once before.
- **Dess ↔ Shelter** (🔴 incident that made her disappear): The incident that made Dess disappear is set in the Shelter area, according to environmental clues gathered in later chapters.
- **Forgotten Man ↔ Shelter** (🟡 same incident): The Forgotten Man hands out eggs near the Shelter area, right where the incident that made Dess disappear is believed to have happened.
- **Carol ↔ Shelter** (🔴 investigates the area): As mayor, Carol officially investigates the Shelter area after Dess's disappearance.
- **Asgore ↔ Shelter** (🔴 secret corkboard): Environmental clue: Asgore appears to have his own secret theory board related to the Shelter, suggesting he too is investigating the area on his own.
- **Lake ↔ Routes** (🟡 Weird Route exclusive ending): The Lake hides an ending exclusive to the Weird Route, only accessible after accumulating enough hidden choices from the Routes system.
- **Lake ↔ Noelle** (🟡 key Ch.5 scene): In Ch.5, Susie takes Noelle into the Lake's water after their date at the Festival — one of the chapter's most talked-about scenes.
- **Onionsan ↔ Normal NPC** (🔴 murder?): Speculative theory: Onionsan's disappearance from the Lake coincides with the Normal NPC's appearance, which some read as a possible causal relationship between the two events.
- **Onionsan ↔ Lake** (🟢 appears at the lake): Onionsan is a recurring NPC who has inhabited the Lake since chapters before Ch.4.
- **Normal NPC ↔ Lake** (🟢 after Onionsan's disappearance): The Normal NPC starts appearing at the Lake right after Onionsan's disappearance.
- **Asriel ↔ Roaring Knight** (🔴 the Knight?): Weak theory that Asriel could be the Roaring Knight under the mask, though his reappearance as a normal Lightner in Ch.4 considerably weakens this idea.
- **Rudy ↔ Roaring Knight** (🔴 the knight?): The same weak theory applied to Rudy, hard to sustain given that he's hospitalized.
- **Carol ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟢 complicity): As mayor, Carol appears to deliberately cover up or ignore clues about the Roaring Knight, which fuels theories about her complicity.
- **Asgore ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟡 kidnapped Ch.5): Asgore is kidnapped by the Roaring Knight in Ch.5, in a direct parallel to Toriel's kidnapping in Ch.3.
- **Kris ↔ Roaring Knight** (🟡 complicity): The Roaring Knight's attacks deal less damage to Kris than to the rest of the group, suggesting some complicity or special bond between them.

## Other
- **Ralsei ↔ Asriel** (🟢 debated theory after Ch.4): After Asriel's return as an independent Lightner, the debate reopens over whether Ralsei is still "part" of him or is now a separate entity.
- **Forgotten Man ↔ Kris** (🟡 Kris's repressed trauma): Some read the Forgotten Man as a manifestation of Kris's repressed trauma — his speech about "the world's dawn" lines up with fragments of the Dreemurr children's childhood that Kris seems to avoid remembering.
- **Forgotten Man ↔ FRIEND** (🟡 eggs/protein): Fan theory: the eggs the Forgotten Man hands out would serve as a food/protein source for FRIEND.
